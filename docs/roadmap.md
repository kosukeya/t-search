# Research Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally provisional. Later executable evidence may revise the ordering, scope, or interpretation of future stages. Detailed histories live in stage-specific protocols, notes, and `results/` checkpoints.

## North-star question

Can block-like/global and becoming-like/local descriptions be related by explicit transformations, and do non-trivial relational structures survive those transformations strongly enough to count as candidate ingredients of physical time without being prematurely identified with time itself?

## Current project-level candidate

`T_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`

with neutral order/succession `O`, admissible perspectives `P`, records/direction/accessibility `R`, Potentiality/extension semantics `V`, and compatibility relations `Xi`.

Stage 7 strengthens the P/O/R core with single-model constrained-quantum evidence. Stage 8 pressure-tests V on the same family. Stage 8A supplies an executable continuation carrier; Stage 8B realizes two modal types on that exact carrier; Stage 8C next tests the full operational/update interface. This remains a structural candidate, not a fundamental ontology of time.

## Stages 0–6 — completed and merged

Stages 0–6 established definitions, classical global/local reconstruction, formal modal underdetermination, reversible record asymmetry, finite Page–Wootters-style relational quantum dynamics, genuine physical clock changes, and the layered candidate `T6_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`.

Potentiality is not defined as quantum randomness, superposition, Born probability, or sampling. Fifty Stage 8 exit criteria are frozen.

`Potentiality != quantum randomness by definition`.

`QExt(e1) = {h_L, h_R}`.

### Stage 8B — typed epistemic and ontic-extension quantum models — completed

Stage 8B uses one `QuantumContinuationCarrier` containing `{h_L,h_R}` and passes the **same carrier object** to both model types.

Epistemic `M_E^Q=(QCarrier,e1,h*,q_E)` stores privileged selected `h*`.

Ontic-extension `M_O^Q(e1)=(QCarrier,e1,QExt(e1),K)` uses a frozen/slots schema with carrier and weights only; the declared schema has no selected/selector/seed/precomputed-outcome/latent-branch/singleton-continuation datum.

Canonical matched weights are `q_E=K=(0.5,0.5)`, generated without consulting `h*`. `EpistemicQuantumPotentiality` and `OnticExtensionQuantumPotentiality` contain the same physical continuation members but remain distinct types.

Swapping epistemic `h_L -> h_R` changes the privileged selected-continuation diagnostic while leaving the Stage 8B pre-discriminating public projection unchanged.

Corrected implementation regression: `595 passed in 140.99s`. Planning/documentation-synchronized regression: `597 passed in 84.13s`.

Stage 8B satisfies criteria 17–21. Criteria 22–50 remain future work.

## Stage 9 — Generally covariant / gravitational extension — deferred gate

The earlier gravity program remains deferred rather than discarded. Possible progression after the finite architecture is sufficiently stable:

`parametrized particle -> simple generally covariant constrained model -> minisuperspace / other tractable gravitational setting`.

Do not project gravitational language backward onto finite toy-model loop residuals or algebraic constraints.

## Stage 10 — Empirical relevance — only if warranted

Seek empirical relevance only after deriving a genuinely discriminating prediction not already guaranteed by the underlying formalism. A useful structural synthesis with no novel empirical prediction remains acceptable.
