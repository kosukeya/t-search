# Research Roadmap

This roadmap is intentionally provisional. Later executable evidence may revise the ordering, scope, or interpretation of future stages. Detailed histories live in stage-specific protocols, notes, and `results/` checkpoints.

## North-star question

Can block-like/global and becoming-like/local descriptions be related by explicit transformations, and do non-trivial relational structures survive those transformations strongly enough to count as candidate ingredients of physical time without being prematurely identified with time itself?

## Current project-level candidate

The provisional finite-model candidate is:

`T_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`

with:

- `O`: neutral order / succession structure;
- `P`: admissible physical perspectives and perspective transformations;
- `R`: records, record-defined direction, and accessibility;
- `V`: Potentiality / extension semantics;
- `Xi`: compatibility conditions among typed layers.

Stage 7 strengthens the P/O/R core with single-model constrained-quantum evidence. Stage 8 now pressure-tests V on the same family. This remains a structural candidate, not a fundamental ontology of time.

### Stage 8A — common quantum-extension substrate — completed

The canonical current anchor is `e1` and:

`QExt(e1) = {h_L, h_R}`.

Both continuations share the same constrained Actuality through `e1` and the same one-bit current record:

`V0=I`, `V1=U_rec`.

They differ only at `e2`:

- `h_L`: `V2=U_rec`;
- `h_R`: `V2=Z_C U_rec`.

`Z_C` is a reversible C-sector phase that acts as identity on memory and commutes with the Stage 7 B-based record target. The future distinction therefore does not define V through R.

Executable evidence:

- future-operator residual `4`;
- canonical normalized `e2` state overlap squared `0`;
- both continuation constructions retain physical dimension `14`;
- all nine A/B/C clock/readout reductions remain rank `14`;
- pure renaming does not enlarge QExt;
- current-prefix-incompatible candidates are rejected;
- canonical terminal behavior is `QExt(e2)=empty`.

This establishes a physical continuation substrate but does not yet instantiate the epistemic/ontic modal distinction.

Stage 8A satisfies exit criteria 11–16. Criteria 17–50 remain future scientific work.

## Stage 9 — Generally covariant / gravitational extension — deferred gate

The earlier plan to jump directly from Stage 6/7 into gravity remains deferred rather than discarded.

Possible progression, only after the finite constrained architecture is sufficiently stable:

`parametrized particle -> simple generally covariant constrained model -> minisuperspace / other tractable gravitational setting`.

Entry conditions include clarity about which layers remain independently needed, non-cheating constraints/interactions, explicit clock/reference domains, and a reason the covariant model can discriminate among surviving candidates.

No gravitational terminology should be projected backward onto finite toy-model loop residuals or algebraic constraints.

---

## Stage 10 — Empirical relevance — only if warranted

Seek empirical relevance only after deriving a genuinely discriminating physical prediction not already guaranteed by the underlying formalism.

A useful structural synthesis with no novel empirical prediction remains an acceptable outcome.

## Fixed questions for every stage

1. What is the global/block-like description?
2. What is the local/perspective-relative description?
3. What maps connect them?
4. Which maps are physically admissible and on what domains?
5. What is invariant, covariant, reconstructible, ambiguous, lost, inaccessible, or representation-dependent?
6. Which claims survive targeted controls and ablations?
7. What is an established finite-model result, what is structural interpretation, and what remains `not_established`?
8. Which next model most strongly discriminates among surviving explanations?

## Cross-cutting methodological cautions

- `random sampling != ontic becoming`;
- `state/configuration equality != event identity`;
- `global reconstructibility != local accessibility`;
- `operational equality != modal/ontological equivalence`;
- `record asymmetry != phenomenal passage`;
- `perspective consistency != temporal succession by definition`;
- `Potentiality != quantum randomness by definition`;

- `future physical inequivalence != modal semantics by itself`;
- `perspective consistency != modal equivalence`;
- `partial-atlas consistency != general covariance`;
- `not_established != false`;
- `finite-model success != empirical discovery`.
