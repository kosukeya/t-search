# Stage 6 Protocol — Candidate Temporal Structure T

Status: **Stage 6.0 protocol freeze**.

Stage 6 does not begin by declaring the Stage 5 perspective atlas to be time itself. It compares the distinct temporal roles that survived Stages 1--5, tests their logical/structural independence with executable witnesses, and asks whether a layered candidate temporal structure can be defined without collapsing order, direction, modality, perspective, accessibility, and operational correspondence into one notion.

## 1. Central question

Stage 6 asks:

> Are the order, perspective, record/direction, Potentiality, accessibility, and operational-correspondence structures encountered in Stages 1--5 different representations of one common temporal structure, or are they partially independent layers constrained by compatibility relations?

The Stage 5 groupoid-like perspective atlas is therefore an input to Stage 6, not its answer.

## 2. Inputs inherited from Stages 1--5

Stage 6 treats the following as previously established **inside their declared toy-model domains**.

### Stage 1 — reconstruction / accessibility

- global relational structure can sometimes be reconstructed from families of local views;
- reconstructibility depends on the declared interface and coverage;
- `global reconstructibility != local accessibility`;
- state/configuration equality does not imply event identity.

### Stage 2 — Potentiality / operational underdetermination

- hidden-selected-future and no-selected-future models can be formally distinct while sharing tested positive-support operational outputs;
- `operational equality != ontological/modal equivalence`;
- `hidden information != absent information`.

### Stage 3 — record-defined temporal orientation

- reversible microscopic dynamics can support asymmetric record structure;
- record orientation reverses under modeled history reversal;
- it cancels under forward/reverse balance;
- it disappears without record coupling or under maximally uncertain memory preparation;
- it can become locally inaccessible while remaining present globally;
- `order != arrow` and `record asymmetry != phenomenal passage`.

### Stage 4 — one-clock relational quantum dynamics

- one ideal finite physical clock supports reversible physical reduction/reconstruction;
- same-clock transition maps satisfy identity/inverse/composition;
- corresponding global/local Born predictions agree in the tested family;
- this does not by itself change the physical clock subsystem.

### Stage 5 — change of physical clock

- multiple ideal internal clock perspectives are connected by reversible support-space maps;
- the maps satisfy pairwise inverse and three-clock composition consistency;
- corresponding state/observable predictions agree when observables are transformed correctly;
- reduced tensor-factor entanglement can remain perspective-dependent;
- the structure survives declared coefficient, dimension, global-phase, permutation, and asymmetric-rate controls;
- the result is a finite groupoid-like perspective atlas, not a fundamental ontology of time.

## 3. Evidence classes

Every Stage 6 claim must be tagged as one of:

1. **Executable witness** — recomputed from an existing Stage 1--5 API or a new Stage 6 construction.
2. **Established toy-model result** — supported by an executable witness and existing regression tests in its declared domain.
3. **Candidate structural interpretation** — a synthesis suggested by multiple witnesses but not itself a physical theorem.
4. **Untested / not established** — no executable witness currently decides it.

A hand-written boolean copied from a previous prose conclusion is not an executable witness.

## 4. Candidate layers — provisional typed inventory

Stage 6 begins with six typed components. They are a hypothesis container, not a claim that all six are fundamental or irreducible.

### 4.1 Order / succession layer `O`

`O` contains declared event carriers and neutral precedence/succession relations.

Representative notation:

`e1 <=_p e2`

for two events/descriptions in perspective `p` when an order relation is actually defined.

This layer does not by itself select a temporal orientation, modal status, or privileged perspective.

### 4.2 Perspective layer `P`

`P` contains admissible perspectives and perspective transformations.

A generic perspective is written:

`p=(frame/view, local coordinate/interface, support/domain metadata)`.

An admissible perspective map is:

`M_{q<-p}: G_p -> G_q`.

Stage 5 supplies one explicit finite quantum instance of this structure.

Perspective arrows are **not** temporal succession arrows.

### 4.3 Record / direction / accessibility layer `R`

`R` contains:

- record variables;
- record-information diagnostics;
- orientation diagnostics such as `A_R`;
- accessibility restrictions.

A sign of a record diagnostic is not identified with neutral order, physical causation, or phenomenal passage.

### 4.4 Potentiality / modality layer `V`

`V` contains possible extensions and the distinction between at least:

- epistemic openness;
- nomological openness;
- ontological openness.

A generic extension structure is written:

`Ext_p(D)`.

Stage 2 requires that operational equality alone cannot collapse distinct modal semantics.

### 4.5 Operational-correspondence layer `Omega`

`Omega` specifies what counts as corresponding measurable/predictive content across perspectives.

Stage 5 supplies the explicit rule:

`state + corresponding observable + perspective map -> same tested prediction`.

`Omega` is not defined as raw numerical equality of all local matrices or all perspective-dependent quantities.

### 4.6 Compatibility layer `Xi`

`Xi` contains compatibility conditions relating the other layers.

This is the primary Stage 6 target. Candidate examples include:

- perspective transport preserving a declared order relation;
- perspective maps commuting with declared temporal/conditioning maps;
- record orientation transforming consistently under event/perspective correspondence;
- modal extension sets transporting consistently across admissible perspective changes;
- operational predictions remaining path-independent on overlapping perspective charts.

## 5. Provisional candidate temporal structure

Stage 6 may use the container:

`T6=(O,P,R,V,Omega;Xi)`.

This notation means only:

> compare these typed structures and their compatibility conditions.

It does **not** mean that Stage 6 has already established a unique temporal object with exactly these irreducible components.

A successful Stage 6 may remove, merge, or split layers if executable ablation/independence evidence supports doing so.

## 6. Two distinct arrow types are mandatory

Stage 6 freezes a strict type distinction.

### Horizontal arrows — perspective transformations

`M_{q<-p}: G_p -> G_q`.

These change the descriptive perspective/frame/interface.

They may be invertible, as in the ideal Stage 5 support maps.

### Vertical arrows — temporal / causal / conditioning relations

A generic vertical relation/map is written:

`D_p(e2<-e1)`

or simply:

`e1 <=_p e2`.

These represent declared succession, conditioning, or causal/order structure inside one perspective/domain.

No result may infer temporal succession merely from the existence of a perspective map.

Frozen guard:

`perspective-change arrow != temporal-succession arrow`.

## 7. Event correspondence is explicit, not numeric-clock equality

When comparing order or records across perspectives, Stage 6 may require an explicit event-correspondence map:

`chi_{q<-p}: E_p -> E_q`.

It may be total, partial, many-to-one, or absent depending on the model.

Equal numeric clock coordinates do not define `chi`.

Frozen guard:

`same clock-coordinate value != same physical event`.

## 8. Compatibility squares

When a meaningful event correspondence and vertical map exist, Stage 6 tests commuting structures of the form:

`M^{e2}_{q<-p} D_p(e2<-e1) ?= D_q(chi(e2)<-chi(e1)) M^{e1}_{q<-p}`.

Equivalently, for order relations:

`e1 <=_p e2  =>  chi(e1) <=_q chi(e2)`

when `chi` is declared orientation-preserving.

If `chi` is declared orientation-reversing, the expected transformed relation must be specified explicitly rather than silently treated as a failure.

A commuting square is evidence of compatibility among typed structures. It is not evidence that the two arrow types are identical.

## 9. Independence / non-implication questions

Stage 6B must distinguish three statuses:

- **refuted** — an executable counterexample exists;
- **supported in declared family** — all declared witnesses satisfy the implication, without claiming universality;
- **not established** — current witnesses do not decide it.

The initial implication matrix must include at least:

1. `order => arrow`;
2. `reversible microdynamics => no record arrow`;
3. `perspective consistency => temporal arrow`;
4. `operational equality => modal/ontological equivalence`;
5. `global reconstructibility => local accessibility`;
6. `perspective-dependent structure => operational inconsistency`;
7. `physical clock change => temporal succession`;
8. `record arrow => ontologically open future`;
9. `Potentiality => phenomenal passage`;
10. `perspective consistency => modal equivalence`.

The expected statuses inherited from prior work are hypotheses for Stage 6B, not hand-coded answers.

## 10. Required executable witness adapters

Stage 6A must derive claims from prior model APIs where possible.

### `W1` — Stage 1 reconstruction/accessibility witness

Recompute at least one case in which global relational structure is reconstructible while a declared local view lacks direct access to that structure.

### `W2` — Stage 2 modal/operational witness

Recompute a matched positive-support case where hidden-selected-future and no-selected-future semantics remain formally distinct while declared operational outputs match.

### `W3` — Stage 3 order/record witness family

Recompute forward-oriented, reversed, balanced, and no-record/uncertain-boundary controls so that neutral order and record orientation can be varied separately.

### `W4` — Stage 4 same-clock transition witness

Recompute same-clock identity/inverse/composition without interpreting these maps as a temporal arrow.

### `W5` — Stage 5 cross-clock witness

Recompute genuine clock-change composition and at least one operational-covariance / perspective-dependent-entanglement case.

Adapters must expose source stage, declared domain, measured quantities, and tolerance.

## 11. Partial perspective atlas protocol

Stage 6C removes the Stage 5 assumption that every direct perspective map is available as a primitive edge.

Represent a perspective atlas as a graph/network:

`A=(Perspectives, admissible direct maps)`.

Required tests:

1. remove one or more direct edges while retaining a connected path;
2. reconstruct an indirect source-to-target map by composition;
3. compare distinct available paths between the same endpoints;
4. verify loop consistency on overlap cycles;
5. distinguish `direct edge absent` from `target perspective nonexistent`;
6. perturb one declared edge and verify that path/loop inconsistency is detected.

For a loop `p0 -> p1 -> ... -> pn=p0`, define a loop residual such as:

`H_loop=||M_{p0<-p_{n-1}} ... M_{p1<-p0} - I_{p0}||`.

In an ideal consistent atlas, `H_loop` must be below tolerance on the declared support.

A nonzero residual in a deliberately perturbed atlas is a consistency failure, not automatically physical curvature or holonomy.

Frozen guard:

`algebraic loop residual != gravitational/physical holonomy unless independently derived`.

## 12. Record transport protocol

Stage 6E must not identify record sign with perspective-map direction.

When record diagnostics are compared across perspectives, the protocol must declare:

- event correspondence `chi`;
- whether `chi` preserves or reverses the modeled history orientation;
- which record variables are corresponding observables/records;
- accessibility restrictions in each perspective.

For an orientation-preserving correspondence, a candidate covariance check is:

`A_R^q(chi(e),Delta')` has the corresponding sign/structure predicted from `A_R^p(e,Delta)`.

For an orientation-reversing correspondence, sign reversal may be the correct covariance rule.

No Stage 6 result may infer phenomenal passage from record transport alone.

## 13. Potentiality transport protocol

Stage 6E treats modality separately from operational correspondence.

If a perspective map induces a map on partial descriptions:

`F_{q<-p}: D_p -> D_q`,

then a candidate modal compatibility condition is a declared correspondence between extension sets:

`F_*(Ext_p(D_p))  ?~  Ext_q(F(D_p))`.

The relation `?~` must be specified as equality, bijection, inclusion, quotient equivalence, or another explicit notion before testing.

Operational equality alone does not imply equality of extension semantics.

A hidden-selected extension and a no-selected-extension model must remain formally distinguishable when the Stage 2 witness says they are.

## 14. Operational correspondence protocol

Stage 6 inherits the Stage 5 rule that corresponding observables, not identical bare matrices, are compared across perspectives.

For a perspective map `M`:

`rho_q = M rho_p M^dagger`,

`O_q = M O_p M^dagger`,

and the tested operational consistency is:

`Tr(rho_p O_p) = Tr(rho_q O_q)`.

Stage 6 may generalize this pattern to non-quantum observables/statistics, but every correspondence map must be explicit.

## 15. Minimality / ablation protocol

Stage 6F tests whether the candidate layered structure contains redundant components.

For each layer `L in {O,P,R,V,Omega}`:

1. remove or neutralize `L` while holding the remaining declared structures fixed where possible;
2. rerun the temporal-role diagnostics;
3. record which capabilities are lost;
4. distinguish `not representable` from `represented but locally inaccessible`;
5. do not claim irreducibility unless a witness shows that the lost role cannot be reconstructed from the retained layers under the declared interface.

Candidate role diagnostics include:

- succession/order;
- perspective transport;
- record-defined direction;
- modal branching/openness semantics;
- cross-perspective operational consistency;
- local accessibility.

The goal is not to maximize the number of layers. The goal is the smallest structure justified by the tested roles and compatibility constraints.

## 16. Stage 6 substage sequence

### Stage 6.0 — protocol freeze

Freeze typed layers, arrow distinctions, evidence taxonomy, witness requirements, partial-atlas tests, compatibility rules, ablation rules, interpretation guards, and exit criteria.

No new temporal ontology is claimed here.

### Stage 6A — structural inventory and executable adapters

- implement typed Stage 6 witness records;
- build adapters to Stages 1--5;
- recompute prior structural witnesses rather than copying prose conclusions;
- produce a machine-readable inventory of what each witness actually measures.

### Stage 6B — independence and countermodel matrix

- evaluate the frozen implication list;
- classify each implication as `refuted`, `supported_in_declared_family`, or `not_established`;
- attach explicit witness IDs to every classification;
- add negative controls against hard-coded truth tables.

### Stage 6C — partial perspective atlas

- remove primitive direct perspective edges;
- reconstruct indirect maps by composition;
- compare alternate paths;
- detect deliberately introduced path/loop inconsistency;
- distinguish absent direct map from absent perspective.

### Stage 6D — horizontal/vertical compatibility

- construct at least one model with both perspective maps and temporal/order/conditioning maps;
- define event correspondences explicitly;
- test commuting squares / order covariance;
- include a deliberate mismatch control;
- keep perspective arrows and temporal arrows typed separately.

### Stage 6E — record and modality transport

- test record-orientation transport under orientation-preserving/reversing correspondences;
- test accessibility dependence;
- define and test at least one explicit modal-extension transport relation;
- preserve Stage 2 operational/ontological underdetermination where appropriate.

### Stage 6F — minimality and ablation

- remove/neutralize candidate layers one at a time where model semantics permit;
- measure which temporal roles disappear;
- identify redundant versus independent structures only from executable witnesses;
- avoid declaring a layer fundamental merely because it is useful.

### Stage 6G — synthesis and Stage 7 gate

- answer the six fixed project questions;
- decide whether evidence supports one minimal `T`, a layered `T`, or a family of complementary temporal structures;
- compare Stage 6 result with the Stage 5 groupoid-like atlas;
- identify the most discriminating Stage 7 physical extension;
- run full regression and merge-readiness review.

## 17. Candidate Stage 7 gates

Stage 6G must choose the next pressure test from evidence rather than chronology alone.

Examples:

- if perspective-map structure dominates but depends on ideal clocks: test interacting/nonideal/POVM clocks;
- if order compatibility is central: move toward parametrized/constrained systems with richer causal structure;
- if records are independently necessary: add explicit memory/record subsystems to a constrained quantum model;
- if modal transport remains independent: design a model that carries both relational quantum perspectives and explicit extension semantics without identifying sampling with ontic becoming.

## 18. Frozen interpretation guards

Stage 6 inherits all prior guards and adds:

- `perspective-change arrow != temporal-succession arrow`;
- `composition-consistent perspective atlas != temporal order`;
- `temporal order != temporal arrow`;
- `record arrow != modal openness`;
- `modal openness != phenomenal passage`;
- `operational correspondence != modal/ontological identity`;
- `equal clock coordinates != event identity`;
- `event correspondence must be declared, not inferred from notation`;
- `partial atlas connectivity != universal physical frame availability`;
- `indirect reconstructibility != direct local accessibility`;
- `loop consistency != fundamental spacetime flatness`;
- `loop inconsistency != physical curvature unless independently derived`;
- `commuting square != identity of arrow types`;
- `one common mathematical encoding != one fundamental physical structure`;
- `ablation usefulness != metaphysical fundamentality`;
- `failure to refute an implication != proof of universal implication`;
- `not established != false`;
- `counterexample inside a declared formal family refutes only the corresponding universal implication under the frozen definitions`;
- `Stage 5 groupoid-like atlas != unique candidate temporal structure`;
- `Stage 6 synthesis != empirical discovery unless a new discriminating physical prediction is derived`.

## 19. Stop / revise conditions

Revise Stage 6 rather than forcing a synthesis if:

- adapters merely hard-code prose conclusions instead of recomputing measurements;
- two arrow types are silently conflated;
- equal numeric clock readings are used as an undeclared event-matching rule;
- a record sign is treated as neutral order or causation by definition;
- modal semantics are inferred from operational equality;
- local inaccessibility is reinterpreted as ontological absence;
- missing direct perspective edges are silently treated as nonexistent perspectives;
- a perturbed atlas residual is labeled physical curvature without a physical derivation;
- a commuting diagram succeeds only because source and target objects were numerically identified without semantic transport;
- an implication table contains statuses without executable witness references;
- `not_established` is collapsed into `refuted`;
- a layer is called fundamental merely because its ablation breaks the current software interface;
- Stage 6 attempts to settle eternalism, becoming, phenomenal passage, or quantum-gravitational time from these finite constructions alone.

## 20. Frozen tolerance and comparison discipline

For inherited floating-point quantum identities, use the existing Stage 4/5 numerical tolerance convention unless a substage explicitly freezes a stricter/looser value with justification.

Symbolic/logical witnesses must not be converted into arbitrary floating-point scores merely to unify APIs.

Each test must report the native evidence type:

- boolean/logical relation;
- rank/dimension;
- graph/order property;
- information-theoretic quantity;
- probability/expectation value;
- numerical operator/vector residual.

## 21. Stage 6 exit criteria

Stage 6 is complete only if all applicable criteria below are satisfied.

### Protocol / typing

1. `O`, `P`, `R`, `V`, `Omega`, and `Xi` are explicitly typed and provisional.
2. perspective arrows and temporal/order arrows are represented by distinct types/interfaces.
3. event correspondence is explicit wherever cross-perspective order/record comparison is performed.
4. evidence classes and implication statuses are machine-readable or otherwise test-auditable.

### Executable inventory

5. Stage 1 reconstruction/accessibility witness is recomputed through an adapter.
6. Stage 2 modal/operational witness is recomputed through an adapter.
7. Stage 3 order/record controls are recomputed through an adapter.
8. Stage 4 same-clock composition witness is recomputed through an adapter.
9. Stage 5 cross-clock composition and operational/perspective-dependent witness is recomputed through an adapter.
10. adapters expose domains, assumptions, measured quantities, and tolerances rather than only verdicts.

### Independence

11. every frozen implication has a status with at least one executable witness reference or an explicit `not_established` reason.
12. at least one executable counterexample confirms `order != arrow`.
13. at least one executable counterexample confirms `operational equality != modal/ontological equivalence`.
14. at least one executable counterexample confirms `reconstructibility != local accessibility`.
15. at least one executable witness confirms `perspective-dependent structure != operational inconsistency` in the tested Stage 5 family.
16. Stage 6 keeps `not established` distinct from `refuted`.

### Partial atlas

17. a perspective atlas with a missing primitive direct edge still reconstructs an indirect map when a valid path exists.
18. alternate valid paths agree within the declared tolerance in an ideal atlas.
19. ideal loop residuals close on the declared support.
20. a deliberately perturbed edge produces a detected path/loop inconsistency.
21. absent direct edge and absent perspective are represented distinctly.

### Horizontal / vertical compatibility

22. at least one model contains both typed perspective maps and typed temporal/order/conditioning relations.
23. at least one declared event correspondence supports a successful compatibility/order-covariance test.
24. at least one deliberate event/map mismatch fails the compatibility test.
25. success of a commuting square is not reported as identity of perspective and temporal arrows.

### Records / modality

26. record transport is tested with explicit orientation-preserving or orientation-reversing correspondence semantics.
27. record accessibility is kept distinct from global record existence.
28. at least one modal-extension transport relation is explicitly typed and tested.
29. Stage 2 operational/modal underdetermination survives unless a new discriminating witness actually breaks it.

### Minimality / synthesis

30. at least three candidate layers are subjected to executable ablation/neutralization tests.
31. ablation results distinguish lost role, inaccessible role, and reconstructible role where applicable.
32. Stage 6 synthesis states whether evidence favors one minimal `T`, a layered `T`, or a family of complementary structures.
33. the strongest result is explicitly separated into toy-model result, structural candidate, and unsupported metaphysical claims.
34. Stage 7 next model is selected by the unresolved/most discriminating Stage 6 pressure test.
35. the full repository regression passes on the final Stage 6 head and merge-readiness review finds no unresolved blockers.

## 22. Strongest statement allowed at Stage 6.0

At protocol freeze, the strongest permitted statement is:

> Stages 1--5 motivate treating temporal order, perspective transformation, record-defined direction/accessibility, modal Potentiality, and operational correspondence as provisionally distinct typed structures whose independence and compatibility should be tested before any one of them is identified with time itself.

Nothing in Stage 6.0 establishes a new temporal ontology or a fundamental temporal invariant.
