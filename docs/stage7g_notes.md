`P != O`, `P != R`, `O != R`

at the level of represented roles in the declared family, together with nontrivial compatibility such as `Xi_PR`.

Frozen boundary:

`functional separation in this model family != metaphysical irreducibility`.

## 7. Why V is selected before richer O or general covariance

This gate has the highest discriminating power because:

- V is the only explicit Stage 6 layer not yet integrated with the Stage 7 P/O/R construction;
- Stage 6 already provides a typed positive P-V compatibility witness;
- Stage 2/6 preserve operational underdetermination between epistemic and ontic Potentiality semantics;
- placing V inside the common quantum construction can test whether those semantics remain genuinely distinct when physical perspective, records, accessibility, and relational history are all present;
- it avoids prematurely identifying quantum superposition, Born support, sampling, or measurement outcomes with ontological future openness.

Richer O remains a strong second gate because Stage 7 uses a deliberately minimal `e0<e1<e2` anchor.  But changing O before integrating V would still leave one whole candidate layer outside the common construction.

POVM/nonideal clocks remain important, but Stage 7D has already exposed one nonideal effect: record interaction deforms the ideal Euclidean atlas into an induced-metric-preserving atlas.

The generally covariant precursor is deferred because it introduces several new physical and interpretive confounds before the finite layered architecture has included V.
