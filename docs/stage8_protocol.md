# Stage 8 Protocol — Quantum Potentiality in the Shared Constrained Construction

Status: **Stage 8.0 protocol frozen before Stage 8A implementation.**

Stage 8 begins after Stage 7 is merged. Stage 7G selected the next evidence gate:

> **Integrate explicit Potentiality / extension semantics `V` into the same constrained quantum construction.**

The project-level candidate entering Stage 8 is:

`T_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`

Stage 7 substantially strengthened the finite-model `P/O/R` core inside one constrained quantum construction. `V` is now the only explicit Stage 6 layer that has not undergone the same single-model pressure test.

This protocol is a specification. Later implementation may revise it only when executable evidence exposes a contradiction, ambiguity, or physically inadmissible assumption; such revisions must be documented rather than silently changing the meaning of Potentiality.

## 1. Central Stage 8 question

> Can two type-distinct Potentiality semantics — epistemic uncertainty about an already-selected quantum continuation and ontic representation of admissible quantum continuations with no selected complete future datum — coexist with the same constrained quantum carrier, genuine clock-perspective changes, record/history structure, and matched operational predictions, while remaining formally distinguishable?

A stronger Stage 8 question follows:

> If the two modal semantics can be represented on one quantum construction, does `V` remain a separate represented role from `P`, `O`, and `R`, or is the selected-versus-unselected continuation distinction reconstructible from the retained quantum state, clock perspectives, records, order/history structure, or Born probabilities?

Stage 8 must allow all of the following outcomes:

- **strengthened** — `V` survives as a distinct represented role compatible with `P/O/R` inside the shared construction;
- **reduced** — the tested `V` role is reconstructible from retained physical structure under the declared interface;
- **broken** — the proposed modal integration is incompatible with the required constrained quantum structure;
- **inconclusive** — typed wrappers can be attached, but no nontrivial physical integration or discriminating test is achieved.

## 2. Non-goals

Stage 8 does **not** establish that:

- physical reality has an ontically open future;
- quantum superposition is Potentiality;
- Born probability is ontic chance in the metaphysical sense;
- collapse, measurement, or sampling is physical becoming;
- the absence of a selected continuation field in one model proves that nature contains no selected future;
- a hidden selected continuation proves eternalism;
- record-defined direction implies ontological openness;
- perspective covariance implies modal equivalence;
- modal extension structure implies phenomenal passage;
- finite constrained quantum consistency implies general covariance;
- any Stage 8 result is a new empirical discovery.

Frozen guards:

- `Potentiality != quantum randomness by definition`;
- `Potentiality != superposition by definition`;
- `Potentiality != Born probability by definition`;
- `Potentiality != absence of measurement by definition`;
- `random sampling != ontic actualization evidence`;
- `Python branch choice != physical becoming`;
- `formal selected-vs-unselected difference != empirical physical difference`;
- `operational quantum equivalence != modal/ontological equivalence`.

## 3. Inherited evidence

### 3.1 Stage 2 modal distinction

Stage 2 separated two model roles on a common branching substrate.

Epistemic history model:

`M_E=(T,h*,q_E)`

where one complete history `h*` is already selected but hidden from the local operational interface.

Ontic extension model:

`M_O(D)=(D,Ext_T(D),K)`

where the current Actuality and admissible continuations are represented, but **no field, hidden selector, seed-derived variable, or equivalent datum singles out one complete future before update**.

The two models can have matched local operational predictions while remaining formally distinct.

Stage 2 therefore established a representational underdetermination result, not an ontological theorem.

### 3.2 Stage 6 modal transport

Stage 6E transported Stage 2 epistemic and ontic extension sets through explicit modal description maps while preserving:

- their type distinction;
- extension-set correspondence;
- matched operational views;
- the presence of selected-history data only in the epistemic model.

A bijective event renaming that failed to preserve extension structure served as a mismatch control.

### 3.3 Stage 7 common quantum carrier

Stage 7 provides the physical substrate into which `V` must now be integrated:

`H_kin^7=H_A tensor H_B tensor H_C tensor H_M`.

The positive record-bearing family contains:

- a common constrained physical space;
- an internally anchored `e0<e1<e2` history;
- reversible target-specific record formation;
- genuine A/B/C clock perspectives;
- induced-metric-preserving clock-change maps;
- explicit event correspondence `chi`;
- record covariance;
- accessibility controls;
- partial-atlas reconstruction;
- a no-record counterfamily retaining tested `P + internal O`.

Stage 7G outcome: **`strengthened`** for the finite-model `P/O/R` core.

## 4. What counts as genuine Stage 8 integration

Stage 8 must not merely place a Stage 2 branching object next to the Stage 7 quantum system.

A candidate quantum continuation must be connected to the Stage 7 carrier by executable physical structure.

At minimum, each continuation must specify a physically admissible completion of the declared current quantum situation and support at least one of the following equivalent implementation forms:

- a future part of an internally clock-anchored schedule acting on the common physical carrier;
- a continuation-specific physical automorphism / constrained propagation map;
- a continuation-specific modified-constraint completion whose physical subspace and reductions can be recomputed;
- another explicit construction that maps the declared current physical data to a distinct future physical completion without relying only on a label.

The Stage 8A baseline should use the least invasive form compatible with Stage 7C/D.

A continuation that differs only by a string label, Python object identity, event renaming, or unused metadata does **not** count as an integrated quantum continuation.

Frozen integration guard:

`typed modal wrapper beside quantum model != quantum-modal integration`.

## 5. Current quantum Actuality

Stage 8 distinguishes the **current physical situation** from a complete future continuation.

The baseline current quantum Actuality is a typed object:

`A_Q(D)=(D, Psi_D, A_D)`

where:

- `D` is the declared internally modeled actual/current event prefix or current anchor, initially through `e1`;
- `Psi_D` is the common constrained physical state restricted to information declared actual at `D`;
- `A_D` is the declared current observable / record interface needed to specify the present physical situation.

The implementation may use an equivalent carrier if Stage 8A shows that a simpler representation is sufficient.

Critical condition:

Two candidate complete continuations used in the baseline must agree on `A_Q(D)` and differ only in their continuation beyond the declared current anchor.

Frozen guard:

`same current Actuality != same complete continuation`.

## 6. Quantum continuation

A **QuantumContinuation** `h` is an executable completion of the declared current quantum Actuality beyond `D`.

Schematically:

`h=(D, Lambda_h, Completion_h)`

where:

- `D` is the common current anchor;
- `Lambda_h` is the continuation-specific future physical map/schedule/constraint completion;
- `Completion_h` contains the resulting physically admissible future structure needed for diagnostics.

The exact Stage 8A dataclass is not frozen here, but its semantics are.

Two continuations are physically equivalent for Stage 8 when they are related only by declared representational equivalence while preserving all currently designated physical labels/observables and continuation diagnostics.

Mere branch renaming does not generate a new possible continuation.

The canonical Stage 8A family must contain at least two inequivalent continuations:

`h_L != h_R`.

Their inequivalence must be executable and not depend solely on names.

## 7. Quantum extension set

Define:

`QExt(D) = { [h] | h is a physically admissible quantum continuation of A_Q(D) }`

where `[h]` denotes the declared continuation-equivalence class.

The baseline must have:

`|QExt(D_0)| >= 2`.

The continuation family should initially share the Stage 7 carrier and current anchor and differ in a future subsystem/observable that is not simply the memory-record variable, so that `V` is not definitionally identified with `R`.

If a record-dependent future branch is used later, it should be introduced as an explicit `R-V` compatibility experiment rather than as the baseline definition of `V`.

## 8. Two typed quantum Potentiality semantics

The same quantum continuation carrier may play different model roles. Those roles must remain type-distinct.

### 8.1 Epistemic quantum Potentiality

Define:

`EPot_Q(D) = { h in QExt(D) | q_E(h | D) > 0 }`.

Interpretation:

> hypotheses about which complete quantum continuation is already selected in the global model but hidden from the declared operational perspective.

The epistemic global model is:

`M_E^Q=(QCarrier, D, h*, q_E)`

with:

`h* in QExt(D)`.

The hidden selected continuation must not be consulted when computing the pre-discriminating local operational prediction.

### 8.2 Ontic-extension quantum Potentiality

Define:

`OPot_Q(D)=QExt(D)`

with weights `K` when a probabilistic operational interface is required.

The ontic-extension model is:

`M_O^Q(D)=(QCarrier, D, QExt(D), K)`.

Critical structural condition:

**No selected complete continuation datum may exist before update.**

This prohibition includes:

- `selected_history` / `selected_continuation` fields;
- hidden branch selectors;
- random seeds stored so as to predetermine a branch;
- latent branch labels used equivalently to a selector;
- precomputed measurement outcomes treated as the selected continuation.

Frozen guard:

`no selected continuation field != proof of ontic openness in nature`.

## 9. Do not equate modal semantics with quantum state representation

The Stage 8 baseline should hold fixed the common current constrained quantum state whenever possible while changing only the global modal semantics.

Neither of these is allowed as a definition:

`ontic Potentiality := coherent superposition over continuation labels`

or:

`epistemic Potentiality := classical mixture over continuation labels`.

Such state representations may later be investigated as candidate physical realizations, but Stage 8 must first preserve the selected-versus-unselected semantic distinction independently of the density-matrix decomposition.

A mixed-state / superposition comparison may be added as a negative/control experiment showing that identical or different density operators do not by themselves settle the selected-future semantic question.

Frozen guards:

- `density matrix decomposition != unique modal semantics`;
- `pure-state superposition != ontic extension semantics by definition`;
- `classical mixture != epistemic selected-future semantics by definition`.

## 10. Operational quantum interface

To compare `M_E^Q` and `M_O^Q` without cheating through Python types or privileged fields, define a shared ontology-neutral operational interface:

`O_Q(G)=(A_Q(D), Next_Q(D), pi_Q(next|D), R_now, Obs_now)`

where the exact implementation may be simplified if redundant.

The interface must expose only declared operationally available data, such as:

- current constrained conditional state/statistics;
- current record diagnostics where applicable;
- admissible immediate continuation outcomes;
- matched next-event / next-readout probabilities;
- declared accessible observables.

It must not expose:

- `h*`;
- model class/type names;
- hidden continuation selector fields;
- privileged ontology diagnostics.

Baseline requirement under matched weights:

`O_Q(G_E(D_0)) == O_Q(G_O(D_0))`

within the declared numerical tolerance.

If equal, report **operational underdetermination under this interface**, not modal identity.

## 11. Probability semantics

### 11.1 Epistemic weights

`q_E(h|D)` means uncertainty over a hidden already-selected continuation.

Immediate operational probabilities are obtained by marginalizing over the live epistemic continuation hypotheses.

### 11.2 Ontic-extension weights

`K(h|D)` or its immediate-event marginal weights admissible continuations in the no-selected-future model.

Stage 8 does not assert a metaphysical interpretation of `K` beyond the model semantics.

### 11.3 Matched baseline

The canonical baseline must choose `q_E` and `K` so that the selected operational interface is equal before discriminating evidence.

A weight-mismatch control must break this equality.

Frozen guard:

`same numerical probabilities != same semantic source of probability`.

## 12. Actualization / update semantics

No random sampling is required for the baseline update experiment.

The next observed event/outcome/continuation evidence must be supplied explicitly to the update API.

### 12.1 Epistemic update

Given new evidence `x`:

1. extend the declared Actuality/current evidence from `D` to `D'`;
2. retain the pre-existing selected continuation `h*`;
3. reject an asserted actual observation inconsistent with `h*` in the canonical run;
4. condition `q_E` on continuations compatible with `D'`;
5. prune epistemic hypotheses accordingly.

### 12.2 Ontic-extension update

Given the same `x`:

1. extend Actuality to `D'`;
2. prune `QExt(D)` to continuations compatible with `D'`;
3. renormalize `K` if required;
4. do not introduce a hidden complete continuation beyond what has become actual.

Thus the update contrast remains:

- epistemic: complete `h*` was already present and remains;
- ontic-extension: Actuality grows while the remaining extension structure stays unselected in the model.

Frozen guards:

- `supplied update event != proof of physical collapse`;
- `model update != phenomenal passage`;
- `simulation order != modeled temporal order`.

## 13. Privileged diagnostics versus operational access

Stage 8 needs privileged tests proving the model states are structurally different.

Allowed test-only diagnostics include:

- epistemic: inspect `selected_continuation(M_E^Q)`;
- epistemic: verify `h*` is physically admissible and belongs to `QExt(D)`;
- ontic-extension: assert that no selected-complete-continuation datum exists;
- compare type-distinct `EPot_Q` and `OPot_Q` wrappers;
- inspect internal extension membership and continuation equivalence.

These diagnostics must not enter `O_Q`.

Frozen distinction:

`formal/internal distinguishability != local operational distinguishability`.

## 14. Genuine clock-perspective transport of V

Stage 8 must upgrade Stage 6E modal event-renaming transport to the genuine Stage 7 clock atlas.

For source perspective `p=(X,j)` and target perspective `q=(Y,k)`, let:

`S_{q<-p}`

be the interacting Stage 7 induced-metric-preserving clock-change map re-derived from the common physical space.

A continuation map/operator represented in source coordinates must be transported by the appropriate similarity/conjugation relation. Schematically:

`Lambda_h^q = S_{q<-p} Lambda_h^p S_{p<-q}`

with the exact domain/codomain and induced metric made explicit in Stage 8D.

The continuation family must satisfy a declared counterpart of:

`F_*(QExt_p(D)) ~= QExt_q(F(D))`.

The exact relation — bijection, partial bijection, or another typed correspondence — must be declared before comparing results.

If event correspondence `chi` is needed, it must be explicit. Equal numeric clock labels do not define continuation identity.

Frozen guards:

- `same branch label across clocks != same continuation`;
- `same bare continuation matrix != corresponding continuation`;
- `P-V covariance != P=V`;
- `perspective consistency != modal equivalence`.

## 15. Record / order / Potentiality compatibility

Stage 8 should keep `V` independent from `R` and `O` at baseline, then test compatibility explicitly.

### 15.1 R-V pressure test

Hold the same Stage 7 record-bearing physical construction fixed while comparing epistemic and ontic-extension Potentiality semantics.

If the same positive record-defined orientation is compatible with both modal semantics under matched operational predictions, then the finite-model implication:

`record-defined direction => ontic no-selected-future semantics`

is refuted in the declared family.

This would **not** prove that real future openness is independent of records.

### 15.2 O-V pressure test

Hold the same internal current/history anchor fixed while changing only the selected-versus-unselected continuation semantics.

If both modal semantics coexist with the same `O`, then:

`internal order/history anchor => unique modal semantics`

is refuted in the declared family.

### 15.3 P-V pressure test

Transport both modal semantics through genuine clock changes while preserving their type distinction and declared extension correspondence.

If successful:

`perspective covariance => modal identity`

is refuted in the declared family.

## 16. Required negative controls

Stage 8 must include, when the relevant positive construction exists:

1. **mere-label continuation control** — different IDs with physically equivalent future action do not count as distinct `QExt` members;
2. **continuation inequivalence control** — canonical `h_L` and `h_R` differ by an executable future physical diagnostic;
3. **hidden-selected-continuation swap** — changing epistemic `h*` alone does not change the pre-discriminating operational view when evidence/weights are fixed;
4. **no-selected-field audit** — ontic-extension model contains no hidden equivalent selector;
5. **weight mismatch** — changing only `q_E` versus `K` can break operational equality;
6. **superposition-is-not-V control** — a coherent branch-label state is not accepted as proof of ontic-extension semantics;
7. **density-matrix ambiguity control** where feasible — physical state representation alone does not uniquely identify modal semantics;
8. **wrong continuation transport** — same bare continuation operator or incorrect similarity map fails covariance where expected;
9. **wrong/misdeclared `chi`** — extension correspondence fails or modal comparison mismatches;
10. **event/continuation renaming invariance** — pure renaming does not create a new physical possible world;
11. **invalid continuation rejection** — constraint-violating or current-prefix-incompatible continuation is excluded from `QExt`;
12. **terminal-current behavior** — `QExt(D_terminal)` has the declared terminal semantics;
13. **record-neutral continuation baseline** — initial `V` distinction does not depend by definition on record-memory differences;
14. **local-access control** where applicable — hiding modal metadata from a perspective is not reported as global absence of the extension structure.

A control may be `not_applicable` only when its positive construction is absent; it may not be silently omitted.

## 17. Integration failure conditions

Stage 8 must be classified as **integration failure / inconclusive** rather than success if any of the following is the strongest achieved result:

- Stage 2 classes are attached as metadata to Stage 7 without executable quantum continuations;
- `QExt` consists only of branch names with no physically distinct future completion;
- ontic Potentiality is defined solely as superposition or Born randomness;
- epistemic Potentiality is defined solely as a mixed density matrix;
- the only distinction is Python type identity and not selected-continuation state structure;
- `h*` leaks into the operational interface;
- ontic model secretly stores an equivalent selector;
- continuation transport is tested only by event-name renaming rather than genuine Stage 7 clock-change maps;
- changing a constraint/continuation family reuses invalid inherited maps without re-derivation;
- V is called covariant without an explicit continuation correspondence.

## 18. Evidence taxonomy

Every Stage 8 claim must be tagged as one of:

1. **Executable witness** — directly recomputed from the declared model;
2. **Established finite-model result** — supported by executable tests inside the declared Stage 8 family;
3. **Candidate structural interpretation** — synthesis across witnesses;
4. **Untested / not established** — no current witness decides the claim.

Do not use hardcoded prose-verdict booleans as substitutes for executable evidence.
