# Stage 8G Notes — Synthesis and Evidence-Selected Stage 9 Gate

Status: **Stage 8G synthesis implemented; final Stage 8 validation is pending the documentation-synchronized CI/review pass.**

## Synthesis choice

The executable Stage 8A–F evidence favors:

`refined_layered`

rather than `reduced`, `broken`, or `inconclusive`.

The top-level bookkeeping candidate remains:

`T8_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`.

Stage 8 does **not** justify replacing the top-level layered architecture with one undifferentiated temporal structure. It does justify refining two layers internally because their named roles show different compatibility, ablation, reconstruction, and accessibility behavior.

Candidate internal typing:

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`

`V=(V_extension,V_semantics,V_weights)`

These component labels are finite-model roles, not claims of fundamental metaphysical primitives.

## Why `refined_layered`

The choice is computed from the following executable evidence:

- `P-O(event effects)` and `P-R(current record)` covariance are positive in Stage 8E;
- `P-V(class/weights)` and `O-V(extension)` compatibility are positive;
- Stage 8F removes current record information while retaining a nontrivial two-continuation P/O/V witness;
- singleton `QExt` removes physical continuation multiplicity while formal selected-vs-unselected typing remains;
- nontrivial continuation weights are not reconstructed from the two-class carrier;
- selected-vs-unselected modal semantics are not uniquely reconstructed from the retained public P/O/current-R structure;
- explicit P-V edge matrices are reconstructible from retained continuation-specific per-node coordinates.

Therefore:

- `R_current` is not required for the declared nontrivial V/P/O witness;
- V physical multiplicity, V modal semantics, and V weights are not one operationally interchangeable role;
- explicit P edge matrices are a derived representation in the current atlas, not evidence that P=V.

## R refinement

Stage 8E and earlier Stage 7 evidence distinguish:

- `R_content`: target-specific record information;
- `R_direction`: lower-vs-upper record-information orientation;
- `R_access`: availability through the declared local readout interface.

The canonical Stage 8 V family has one-bit record content but zero directional record score. Hidden access keeps the global record while local access becomes inaccessible.

Thus:

`record content != directional record arrow != local record accessibility`.

## V refinement

Stage 8F distinguishes:

- `V_extension`: physical continuation multiplicity / `QExt` structure;
- `V_semantics`: selected-complete-continuation versus no-selected-continuation typing;
- `V_weights`: continuation weight/measure assignment.

A singleton carrier loses physical multiplicity but retains formal modal typing and reconstructs its only normalized weight as 1. A two-class carrier admits distinct nontrivial weights with distinct predictions, while public P/O/current-R data do not uniquely reconstruct selected-vs-unselected semantics.

Thus the current evidence favors an internally structured V layer, without claiming that these subroles are fundamental primitives.

## Remaining unresolved claims

Stage 8 still does not establish:

- `directional_record_structure <=> nontrivial_V_structure`;
- `record_defined_direction => ontological_future_openness`;
- selected-vs-unselected formal semantics as an empirical fact about nature;
- `Potentiality => phenomenal_passage`;
- `physical_clock_change => temporal_succession`;
- `P-V covariance => general covariance`;
- full Stage 8C cross-continuation measurement-family covariance;
- fundamental independence of the internal V roles;
- a unique or fundamental ontology of time.

`not_established != false`.

The executable ranking is designed to select the most discriminating unresolved pressure test, not the most ambitious extension.

Expected ranking from the current evidence:

1. `directional_record_potentiality` — score 9;
2. `full_measurement_covariance` — score 6;

Selected Stage 9 gate:

This is selected because Stage 8E's strongest remaining partial row is full `P/O/R_direction/V`: the canonical nontrivial V family contains current record content but no directional record arrow, while Stage 7C already provides a constrained directional-record control with the same current prefix.

## Stage 9 design pressure

Stage 9 should construct a nontrivial `QExt` whose members share the declared current Actuality but whose later internally anchored dynamics include directional record formation. It should then test:

- whether `R_direction` and nontrivial `V_extension` coexist in one constrained family;
- whether directional record statistics covary under genuine continuation-aware clock changes;
- forward / reversed / balanced / no-record controls;
- whether selected-vs-unselected `V_semantics` remains operationally underdetermined when directional R is present;
- whether record direction constrains V weights or continuation classes;
- whether any relation between record direction and modal openness is actually derived rather than assumed.

Frozen guard for Stage 9:

`directional record arrow != ontological future openness`.

## Why gravity is deferred again

The generally covariant / gravitational precursor is not abandoned. It is ranked below the directional-R/V gate because two finite-model issues remain explicit: full directional P/O/R/V integration is partial and full Stage 8C measurement-family covariance is not established. Introducing covariance/gravity now would add new constraint and reference-frame confounds before these remaining finite links are isolated.
