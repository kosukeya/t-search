## 1. Frozen central question

Can one constrained quantum continuation family carry nontrivial `V` and genuinely directional `R_direction`, preserve their typing under genuine clock changes, and keep selected-versus-unselected future semantics empirically testable rather than assumed from the arrow?

Stage 9 does not assume that success means ontological becoming.

`continuation identity != record-direction identity`.
