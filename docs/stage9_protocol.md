# Stage 9 Protocol — Directional Records with Nontrivial Quantum Potentiality

Status: **Stage 9.0 protocol frozen; Stage 9A and Stage 9B completed; Stage 9C next.**

Selected Stage 9 gate from Stage 8G:

> **Integrate directional record formation with nontrivial quantum Potentiality in one constrained continuation family.**

Current refined candidate carried into Stage 9:

`T9_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`

with tested internal role typing:

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`

`V=(V_extension,V_semantics,V_weights)`.

These labels are finite-model bookkeeping roles. Stage 9 does not assume that they are fundamental or metaphysically irreducible.

## 1. Central question

Stage 8 placed nontrivial quantum Potentiality `V` in the same constrained multi-clock construction as `P/O/current-R`, but its canonical continuation family had one-bit current record content and zero directional record score. Stage 7C/8E separately supplied a directional-record completion with the same minimal order/current prefix.

Stage 9 asks:

> Can a single constrained quantum continuation family carry both nontrivial `V` and a genuinely directional `R_direction`, with the two structures remaining correctly typed and perspective-consistent under genuine clock changes, while selected-versus-unselected future semantics remain testable rather than being assumed from the record arrow?

The target is a same-family pressure test of:

`R_direction <-> V`.

Stage 9 does **not** assume that a positive result means ontological becoming.

## 2. Strong integration criterion

A canonical Stage 9 current anchor `D_*` must satisfy all of the following in the same declared family:

1. `QExt(D_*)` contains at least two physically inequivalent admissible quantum continuations;
2. the continuations share the declared current Actuality/prefix through `D_*`;
3. each canonical continuation supports a nonzero directional-record diagnostic at the same declared current anchor;
4. the canonical continuation family gives a coherent direction rather than obtaining the sign only by averaging branch-dependent opposite arrows;
5. the physical distinction between continuation classes is not definitionally the memory/record label used to diagnose `R_direction`;
6. no selected complete continuation is inserted into the ontic-extension model before update.

Canonical target:

`|QExt(D_*)| >= 2`

and for every canonical `h in QExt(D_*)`, a nonzero `A_R(h,D_*)` before continuation weighting.

## 3. Why per-continuation direction is required

Stage 9 distinguishes:

- `A_R(h,D_*)`: continuation-specific structural record-direction score;
- `A_R^mix(D_*)`: optional model-level score after weighting continuations by `q_E` or `K`.

The canonical positive witness establishes direction before model-level mixture.

`weighted directional score != continuation-independent directional structure`.

## 4. Canonical construction — Stage 9A result

The Stage 9A constrained schedules are:

- `h_L: (V_0,V_1,V_2)=(I,U_rec,U_scr U_rec)`;
- `h_R: (V_0,V_1,V_2)=(I,U_rec,Z_C U_scr U_rec)`.

They retain:

- `QExt(e1)={h_L,h_R}`;
- shared normalized e0/e1 A-perspective states;
- nonzero same-sign per-continuation direction before weighting;
- physical dimension 14;
- rank-14 reductions for all tested A/B/C clock readings;
- a branch distinction carried by the memory-neutral and record-target-neutral future C-sector action `Z_C`.

Thus Stage 9A establishes substrate-level coexistence of nontrivial `V_extension` and `R_direction`.

## 5. Directional record semantics

Stage 9 does not define a record as “information about the past”. For the declared current anchor and lower/upper comparison targets:

`A_R = I(R_* ; X_lower) - I(R_* ; X_upper)`

`A_acc = Acc(R_* -> X_lower) - Acc(R_* -> X_upper)`.

The lower/upper labels are neutral event-correspondence labels before the diagnostic is evaluated.

A nonzero mutual-information contrast is not sufficient by itself; provenance of the record/scramble interaction and target-observable typing remain explicit.

## 6. Direction is distinct from content and access

Stage 9 preserves:

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`.

Questions remain distinct:

- target-specific record information (`R_content`);
- signed orientation (`R_direction`);
- local readout availability (`R_access`).

`record content != directional record arrow`.

Stage 9B adds a concrete example: the balanced control can retain symmetric information on both sides while `A_R=0`.

`balanced zero R_direction != no R_content`.

## 7. Potentiality semantics carried forward

Epistemic:

`M_E^Q=(QCarrier,D_*,h*,q_E)`

where one complete continuation `h*` is already selected globally but hidden from the declared operational interface.

Ontic-extension:

`M_O^Q(D_*)=(QCarrier,D_*,QExt(D_*),K)`

where admissible continuations and weights are represented but **no selected complete continuation datum exists before update**.

The presence, reversal, or removal of a directional record does not alter these definitions by fiat.

Stage 9C must test whether the formal distinction remains underdetermined by the declared public directional interface when numerical weights are matched.

## 8. Ontology-neutral directional operational interface

Stage 9C should define a typed interface extending Stage 8C:

`O_QR=(D_*,rho_now,R_now,R_dir_access,Next_Q(D_*),pi_Q(next|D_*),observed_outcome)`.

`R_dir_access` may contain only directional statistics or observables granted by the declared interface. Privileged access to a hidden selected continuation is forbidden. Matched epistemic and ontic-extension models must be compared through `O_QR`, not by Python type or hidden fields.

## 9. Directional controls — Stage 9B result

Stage 9B implements all four frozen controls on the same or explicitly corresponding constrained carrier family.

### Forward

Canonical Stage 9A schedules are retained. Both h_L and h_R give:

`(A_R,A_acc)=(+1,+0.5)`.

### Reversed

The common directional interaction skeleton is reversed at the modeled-history level:

`(I,U_rec,U_scr U_rec) -> (U_scr U_rec,U_rec,I)`.

This is not a reversed Python iteration and is not a post-hoc sign negation. The independent V branch action stays on the e2 side so nontrivial `V_extension` remains future-facing.

Both continuations give:

`(A_R,A_acc)=(-1,-0.5)`

with:

`A_R^rev = -A_R^fwd`

and matching accessibility sign covariance.

### Balanced

Balanced is the equal operational mixture of the forward and reversed constrained histories for a fixed continuation. It gives:

`A_R=0`

`A_acc=0`.

It is not represented as an averaged unitary dressing.

`balanced mixture != pure constrained history`.

### No-record

No-record neutralizes the record write while retaining the scrambler and h_L/h_R continuation distinction:

- h_L: `(I,I,U_scr)`;
- h_R: `(I,I,Z_C U_scr)`.

It gives:

`A_R=0`

`A_acc=0`.

All four controls retain nontrivial h_L/h_R `V_extension`. Forward, reversed, and no-record remain valid pure constrained histories with physical dimension 14 and minimum A/B/C reduction rank 14; balanced is a mixture of separately valid pure histories.

`control of R_direction != control of V_semantics`.

## 10. Branch distinction must not be the arrow

The continuation classes remain physically inequivalent for a reason independent of the directional record label:

`continuation identity != record-direction identity`.

The same C-sector branch distinction survives positive, negative, balanced-zero, and no-record-zero directional controls. A construction in which branch identity itself encodes arrow sign would not satisfy Stage 9.

## 11. Genuine clock-change requirement

For every canonical continuation `h`, Stage 9D must re-derive the physical A/B/C clock reductions and reconstructions from that continuation's constrained carrier.

Perspective maps remain:

`S^h_{Y<-X}(k,j)=R^h_Y(k)E^h_X(j)`.

Required checks include:

- rank/support validity for every declared chart;
- direct-global route consistency;
- inverse/round-trip consistency;
- three-clock composition consistency;
- correct transport of record observables and V class/weight data;
- explicit event correspondence and continuation/class correspondence.

Do not reuse a map derived for one continuation or coordinate convention without proving it is valid for the target continuation.

## 12. Record-observable typing guard

Stage 8E exposed a failure mode in which a mathematically covariant observable could still be semantically wrong in the wrong coordinate/support basis.

`covariance of a wrongly typed observable != semantic correctness`.

Every directional record observable must retain source support, continuation, perspective/clock, event anchor, coordinate basis, and target/register semantics.

## 13. Event and class correspondence

Equal numeric clock readings do not identify physical events. Stage 9 must declare an event correspondence such as:

`chi_{Y<-X}^h : E_X^h -> E_Y^h`

and a continuation/class correspondence such as:

`h_L -> h_L`, `h_R -> h_R`.

Removing or corrupting these correspondences is a required negative control.

`equal numeric clock readings != event identity`.

`bare matrix covariance != physical event/class correspondence`.

## 15. Modal underdetermination test with direction present

Stage 9C/E must repeat the hidden-selected-versus-no-selector comparison on the directional carrier.

Minimum checks:

- same physical continuation carrier;
- same current Actuality;
- same accessible directional record interface;
- matched positive continuation weights;
- hidden `h*` swap does not change the declared pre-discriminating `O_QR` view;
- changing only weights changes prediction where the interface is weight-sensitive;
- the ontic-extension object contains no selected complete continuation datum;
- directional record structure remains present in both typed models.

A matched result supports operational underdetermination in the declared family. It does not prove that the future is ontically open or fixed.
