Status: **Stage 9.0 protocol frozen; Stage 9A, Stage 9B, Stage 9C, and Stage 9D completed; Stage 9E next.**

Current refined candidate:

`T9_candidate=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`

## 1. Frozen central question

Can one constrained quantum continuation family carry nontrivial `V` and genuinely directional `R_direction`, preserve their typing under genuine clock changes, and keep selected-versus-unselected future semantics empirically testable rather than assumed from the arrow?

Stage 9 does not assume that success means ontological becoming.

## 2. Strong integration criterion

At one common current anchor `D_*`:

`|QExt(D_*)| >= 2`

with physically inequivalent admissible continuations sharing the current Actuality, and for every canonical continuation `h` a nonzero:

`A_R(h,D_*)`

before any continuation weighting.

The continuation distinction may not be defined by the record-direction label/channel.

`continuation identity != record-direction identity`.

`weighted directional score != continuation-independent directional structure`.

## 3. Canonical Stage 9A carrier — completed

At e1:

`QExt(e1)={h_L,h_R}`.

Schedules:

- `h_L: (I,U_rec,U_scr U_rec)`;
- `h_R: (I,U_rec,Z_C U_scr U_rec)`.

The continuations share e0/e1 Actuality, have physical dimension `14`, retain rank-14 A/B/C reductions, and are physically inequivalent at e2. The `Z_C` continuation action is memory-neutral and record-target-neutral.

Every continuation separately carries the same lower-index directional structure before weighting.

## 5. Stage 9B directional controls — completed

The continuation distinction is held fixed while the record-direction construction is changed.

### Forward

For both h_L and h_R:

`(+1,+0.5)` for `(A_R,A_acc)`.

### Reversed

The common interaction history is reversed:

`(I,U_rec,U_scr U_rec) -> (U_scr U_rec,U_rec,I)`.

`(-1,-0.5)`.

`reversed diagnostic sign != reversed Python iteration`.

### Balanced

`(A_R,A_acc)=(0,0)`.

`balanced mixture != pure constrained history`.

### No-record

Record write removed while h_L/h_R `V_extension` remains:

- h_L: `(I,I,U_scr)`;
- h_R: `(I,I,Z_C U_scr)`.

Again `(A_R,A_acc)=(0,0)`.

`control of R_direction != control of V_semantics`.

## 6. Stage 9C modal semantics — completed

Epistemic model:

`M_E^QR=(QRCarrier,D_*,h*,q_E)`

with one complete continuation selected but hidden from the public interface.

Ontic-extension model:

`M_O^QR(D_*)=(QRCarrier,D_*,QExt(D_*),K)`

where admissible continuations and weights are represented and **no selected complete continuation datum exists before or after** the declared evidence update.

The public interface is:

`O_QR=(D_*,rho_now,R_now,R_dir_access,Next_QR(D_*),pi_QR(next|D_*),observed_outcome)`.

With `q_E=K=(0.5,0.5)`, the two public views are equal despite distinct privileged modal structures. Swapping hidden `h*` leaves `O_QR` unchanged. A weight control `K=(0.75,0.25)` changes future prediction but preserves current density, record content, and direction.

`operational directional equality != modal/ontological identity`.

`hidden h* diagnostic != operational access to h*`.

`weight sensitivity != selected-continuation observability`.

`control of V_weights != determination of V_semantics`.

`explicit evidence update != ontological becoming`.

`underdetermined != ontically open`.

## 7. Stage 9D genuine clock transport — completed

Each h_L/h_R constrained completion receives a separately re-derived A/B/C atlas. For continuation `h` and clock chart `(X,j)` define support coordinates `C_{h,X,j}` and the induced metric:

`G_{h,X,j}=C_{h,X,j}^{-dagger} C_{h,X,j}^{-1}`.

The genuine clock map is equivalently represented as:

`S^h_{Y<-X}(k,j)=R^h_Y(k)E^h_X(j)`

and in support coordinates:

`S^h_{Y,k<-X,j}=C_{h,Y,k} C_{h,X,j}^{-1}`.

- minimum chart rank `14`;

- wrong event, class, continuation-map, and bare-observable controls are rejected.

## 8. Record-observable typing guard

Every directional record observable retains explicit:

- continuation;
- perspective/clock;
- clock reading;
- event anchor;
- relational target;
- register semantics;

- matrix representation.

`covariance of a wrongly typed observable != semantic correctness`.

## 9. Event and class correspondence

Equal clock numbers do not identify physical events.

The positive event correspondence preserves `e0/e1/e2`. An explicit reversing correspondence swaps e0 and e2 while fixing e1. A reversing map falsely declared preserving is rejected.

`equal numeric clock readings != event identity`.

The positive continuation/class correspondence is:

`h_L -> h_L`, `h_R -> h_R`.

Swapped classes and a mapping that declares terminal e2 to be the current e1 event are rejected.

`bare matrix covariance != physical event/class correspondence`.
