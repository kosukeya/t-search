## Purpose

Stage 9E does not add a new physical carrier or a new metaphysical assumption. It synthesizes the executable evidence accumulated in Stages 9A–9D and classifies the six compatibility questions frozen in the Stage 9 protocol:

1. `R_direction` with `V_extension`;
2. `R_direction` with `V_weights`;
3. `R_direction` with `V_semantics`;
4. `R_access` with `V`;
5. `P-R_direction-V`;
6. `O-R_direction-V`.

The central methodological distinction is:

`compatibility != identity != determination`.

A positive compatibility result says that typed roles coexist consistently in the declared finite family. It does not say that one role reduces to another, that they are universally independent, or that a metaphysical interpretation has been established.

`QExt(e1)={h_L,h_R}`

with the same canonical nonzero direction before continuation weighting.

Stage 9B strengthens this result. The same h_L/h_R continuation distinction survives:

- reversed negative direction;
- balanced zero direction;
- no-record zero direction.

Thus nontrivial `V_extension` and `R_direction` are compatible in the declared family, and neither continuation identity nor directional sign is definitionally the other.

The corresponding determination claims are stronger and are refuted in this finite family:

`V_extension identity => unique R_direction` — **implication_refuted**;

`R_direction => unique V_extension identity` — **implication_refuted**.

The first follows because the same continuation distinction survives positive, negative, and zero directional controls. The second follows because physically inequivalent h_L and h_R carry the same canonical direction.

This is not a universal R–V independence theorem.
