Project finalization decision: **freeze new scientific search after merged Stage 16 and enter a documentation/synthesis-only finalization phase**.

`project closure decision != new scientific evidence`.

## Central research question being closed

The project has progressively tested whether differences among global/block-like, local/becoming-like, clock-relative, gauge-relative, path-dependent, and basis-dependent descriptions contain a physically meaningful remainder that cannot be removed by declared reconstruction, quotient, clock, gauge, path-compensation, or basis transformations.

A compact closure formulation of the central question is:

> Can the project identify a representation-independent physical difference that discriminates blockness from ontological becoming, rather than merely constructing descriptions that look more block-like or more becoming-like?

The finalization phase does not assume that blockness and becoming are exhaustive metaphysical alternatives, nor that either term already has a unique physically privileged formalization.

## Why new search is frozen after Stage 16

### 1. Search-family regress

Stage 16D established a bounded result: a global strongly commuting basis exists, while no local strongly commuting witness was found in the declared L0 / explicit one-step L1 / depth<=4 elementary-shear / frozen translation-covariant affine cyclic L1 searches.

The unresolved question is whether that negative local-witness result reflects a genuine structural obstruction or the incompleteness of the declared search class.

A direct continuation can always enlarge the class again: greater composition depth, higher-degree coefficient families, weaker symmetry assumptions, or broader admissible variable dependence. Repeating that procedure risks producing an indefinitely nested sequence of claims of the form

`no witness found in declared class X`

without approaching a full-class theorem or a physically privileged admissibility principle.

The project therefore freezes **search expansion as an end in itself**.

`larger bounded search != general nonexistence theorem`.

### 2. Admissibility is not fixed by the finite search alone

Any statement that a structure is physically invariant requires a prior answer to:

`invariant under which transformations?`

Stage 16 supplied a careful finite-graph notion of L0/L1/Lfinite basis locality, but the project has not established that this declared class is the unique or physically fundamental equivalence class relevant to blockness/becoming.

Making the class narrower can manufacture apparent obstructions; making it broader can remove them. The finite search itself cannot, without an additional physical principle, determine which redescription class is ontologically privileged.

`declared basis locality != uniquely privileged physical equivalence`.

### 3. Finite-model extrapolation boundary

The validated carriers are finite precursors. They are useful for separating algebraic, quotient, clock, path, and basis effects, but they do not establish continuum spacetime, relativistic microcausality, the hypersurface-deformation algebra, or general relativity.

Consequently, even a stronger finite C4 obstruction would require an independent argument before it could be interpreted as a fundamental spacetime obstruction.

`finite graph locality != relativistic locality`.

`finite constraint precursor != general relativity`.

### 4. Operational/ontological gap

The project has repeatedly established operational descent, covariance, quotient consistency, and future-measurement compatibility on declared finite families. These are statements about representational and operational structure.

They do not by themselves establish whether the future is actual, whether becoming is ontologically fundamental, or whether two operationally equivalent descriptions are ontologically equivalent.

`operational equivalence != ontological equivalence`.

`future-measurement covariance != future actuality`.

### 5. Missing bridge criterion for ontological becoming

Even if a future theorem established that a declared class cannot locally Abelianize the closed-cycle carrier, the project currently lacks a justified bridge principle of the form

`local non-Abelianizability => ontological becoming`.

The same applies to noncommutativity, path ordering, relational change, structure functions, or compensated path differences. Each can be mathematically meaningful without constituting a sufficient criterion for ontological becoming.

`failure to Abelianize != ontological becoming`.

`relational change != ontological becoming by definition`.

## Closure judgment

The project therefore adopts the following project-level judgment:

> After Stage 16, the expected discriminating gain from further bounded basis-search expansion is too small relative to the unresolved admissibility, finite-model, and operational-to-ontological bridge problems. The current research program is better concluded by auditing and synthesizing what Stage 1–16 establish, do not establish, and leave underdetermined than by extending the same search pattern indefinitely.

This judgment does **not** assert that no deeper theorem, invariant, obstruction, or constructive local witness exists. It asserts only that such a result is not presently warranted by continuing the existing bounded-search trajectory without an additional theoretical principle.

## What remains unresolved at closure

The final synthesis must preserve at least the following unresolved alternatives:

- the Stage 16 closed-C4 local-witness failure may reflect a genuine structural obstruction;
- it may reflect incompleteness of the declared search family;
- it may depend on the declared basis-locality notion;
- it may be peculiar to the finite carrier;
- a broader theory may reveal a useful cycle invariant or obstruction class;
- a broader admissible transformation may instead exhibit a local strongly commuting witness.

These alternatives remain **underdetermined**, not false.

`not_established != false`.

## Finalization scope

The finalization phase is restricted to:

1. reconstructing the central research question and evidential chain across Stages 1–16;
2. distinguishing established, bounded, not-established, and underdetermined claims;
3. identifying which candidate indicators of becoming failed to provide sufficient ontological discrimination;
4. recording the methodological limits that prevent stronger conclusions;
5. synchronizing README/roadmap with the actual merged Stage 16 and project-closure state;
6. running a final full-repository regression and merge-readiness audit for the closure-only delta.

The finalization phase must **not** add a new scientific carrier, enlarge the Stage 16 search class, introduce Stage 17 scientific evidence, or reinterpret documentation edits as new evidence.

## Reopening rule

The project may be reopened only if a materially new discriminator is supplied, such as:

- a physically motivated and independently justified admissibility/equivalence principle;
- a theorem or invariant applying to a clearly defined full transformation class rather than another ad hoc finite search enlargement;
- a constructive witness that directly overturns a currently underdetermined structural possibility;
- a new physical bridge principle connecting a validated invariant to blockness/becoming with independently defensible assumptions;
- a continuum or established-physics embedding that changes the evidential status of the finite precursors.

Merely increasing search depth, polynomial degree, sample size, or candidate count is not by itself a reopening condition.
