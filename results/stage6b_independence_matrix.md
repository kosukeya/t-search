## Strongest supported Stage 6B statement

**Within the declared Stage 1--5 toy-model families, executable countermodels show that neutral order does not force record-defined direction; microscopic reversibility does not exclude record-defined direction; tested operational equality does not force formal modal/model equivalence; global reconstructibility does not force accessibility through one declared local interface; and perspective-dependent reduced structure does not force operational inconsistency when corresponding observables are transported correctly. By contrast, the current witnesses do not decide whether perspective consistency implies a temporal arrow or modal equivalence, whether physical clock change implies temporal succession, whether record direction implies an ontologically open future, or whether Potentiality implies phenomenal passage.**

This is a bounded structural result, not a universal theory of time.

## Structural implication for the Stage 6 hypothesis

The result makes a simple collapse of all candidate temporal roles less plausible inside the tested framework.

At minimum, the existing witnesses require the analysis to keep apart:

- neutral order from record-defined direction;
- microscopic reversibility from informational orientation;
- global reconstructibility from local accessibility;
- operational correspondence from modal/model semantics;
- perspective-dependent reduced structure from operational consistency.

This does not yet prove that `T6=(O,P,R,V,Omega;Xi)` is the minimal or final structure. It supports continuing the Stage 6 program as a compatibility problem among typed layers rather than identifying one surviving structure with time by declaration.

## Evidence boundary

The five `not_established` rows are scientifically informative gaps. They identify exactly where the current adapters cross from a measured layer into an unmeasured one.

Stage 6 therefore still needs models that combine layers explicitly before those implications can be decided. In particular, later substages must not infer cross-layer facts from vocabulary alone.

## Anti-hard-coding control result

The evaluator changes classification when input measurements change:

- making the W1 remote event locally accessible changes I5 from `refuted` to `supported_in_declared_family`;
- disabling W1 global reconstruction changes I5 to `not_established`;
- forcing the W5 Born residual above tolerance changes I6 from `refuted` to `supported_in_declared_family`.

Thus the matrix is evidence-driven rather than a fixed expected-answer mapping.
