## Central question

Can the Stage 2/6 selected-versus-unselected Potentiality distinction be represented on physically admissible future continuations of the Stage 7 constrained quantum carrier, remain operationally underdetermined under a matched interface, and transform consistently under genuine clock changes without being identified with quantum randomness by definition?

## Frozen modal distinction

Epistemic quantum model:

`M_E^Q=(QCarrier,D,h*,q_E)`

where one complete continuation `h*` is already selected globally but hidden from the declared operational interface.

Ontic-extension quantum model:

`M_O^Q(D)=(QCarrier,D,QExt(D),K)`

where physically admissible continuations are represented but no selected complete continuation datum exists before update.

The two models may share the same numerical continuation weights and operational predictions while remaining structurally distinct.

## Frozen QExt semantics

`QExt(D)` is not a set of branch labels.

It is the set of declared equivalence classes of **physically admissible quantum continuations** beyond a shared current quantum Actuality.

Each canonical continuation must contain executable future physical structure acting on the Stage 7 carrier — for example a future clock-anchored schedule completion, physical automorphism, or re-derived constrained completion.

Stage 8A must produce at least two physically inequivalent continuations sharing the same current Actuality.

## Integration requirement

- the common constrained physical carrier;
