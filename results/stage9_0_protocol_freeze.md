## Central question

Can one constrained multi-clock quantum continuation family support, at one declared common current anchor, both:

- nontrivial physically inequivalent `QExt(D_*)`; and
- a coherent nonzero `R_direction` in every canonical continuation,

while preserving the Stage 8 selected-versus-unselected modal distinction and genuine clock-perspective consistency?

## Strong canonical integration requirement

The future implementation must establish, before weighted aggregation:

`|QExt(D_*)| >= 2`

and, for every canonical continuation `h`:

`A_R(h,D_*) > 0`

or the corresponding common nonzero sign under an explicitly declared orientation convention.

The continuation classes must differ through a physical degree of freedom that is not definitionally the record-direction label or memory register used to diagnose the arrow.

Thus:

`continuation identity != record-direction identity`.

## Frozen construction strategy

The preferred Stage 9A construction combines the already-tested ingredients rather than starting from an unrelated model:

1. retain the Stage 8 constrained multi-clock carrier and shared current anchor;
2. retain/restore the Stage 7C-style reversible record-writing plus later target-scrambling mechanism needed for a directional record contrast;
3. preserve at least two future physical continuation classes after the common anchor;
4. keep the continuation-defining future action memory-neutral and record-target-neutral where feasible;
5. re-derive each continuation's A/B/C constrained clock atlas rather than reusing maps by assumption.

This is a design target, not a positive Stage 9 result.

## Frozen R and V typing

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`

and:

`V=(V_extension,V_semantics,V_weights)`.

A one-bit current record is not sufficient for `R_direction`, and nontrivial continuation multiplicity is not sufficient for ontic openness.
