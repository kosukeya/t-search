## Frozen status vocabulary

- `established_on_declared_family` — positively established on an explicitly declared finite/model family.
- `bounded_negative_result` — a negative result or class-level exclusion only inside an explicitly declared restricted search/transformation class.
- `not_established` — the merged evidence does not establish the claim; this is not a proof of falsity.
- `underdetermined` — multiple live interpretations remain compatible with the merged evidence.
- `methodological_judgment` — a project-level decision or synthesis rule, not a new physical claim.

`not_established != false`.

## Reading rules

1. A finite counterexample licenses a bounded non-implication inside the declared family; it does not automatically license a universal theorem.
2. A representation/basis/gauge change that preserves tested quotient or operational content is evidence against treating the changed presentation feature as automatically physical; it is not by itself an ontological theorem.
3. A negative search is never silently upgraded from `no witness in declared class` to `no witness exists`.
4. Positive finite-model covariance, reconstruction, or descent is never silently upgraded to general covariance, general relativity, eternalism, or ontological becoming.
5. Historical Stage 1–16 wording remains authoritative at its declared scope; this ledger may clarify scope but may not strengthen it.
