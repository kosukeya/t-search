# 1. Research question

The central question of t-search was not whether one can write down a mathematical description that looks block-like, nor whether one can write down a local description that looks becoming-like. Both are easy to construct.

The stronger question was:

> **Can one identify a representation-independent physical difference that discriminates blockness from ontological becoming, rather than merely constructing descriptions that look more block-like or more becoming-like?**

Across the project, this question was approached by progressively changing the descriptive interface while asking what survived:

- global versus local descriptions;
- complete versus partial information;
- hidden-selected versus no-selected-future semantics;
- record-bearing versus record-neutral descriptions;
- one internal clock versus another;
- external parameterization changes;
- gauge representative changes;
- different constraint-generated paths;
- different constraint bases;
- locality-preserving versus unrestricted basis transformations;
- open-chain versus closed-cycle spatial indexing.

The hoped-for discriminator would have had to survive these representation changes in a way that could not be explained merely by bookkeeping, gauge redundancy, basis choice, incomplete search, or the chosen finite carrier.

The project never assumed that blockness and becoming exhaust all possible metaphysical positions. It also never assumed that a global mathematical description is a physically realized God's-eye point of view, or that a local update description is ontological becoming by definition.

The final conclusion must therefore be read as an evidence audit about what the implemented program can and cannot discriminate, not as a forced verdict between two exhaustive metaphysical doctrines.

# 2. Methodological strategy

The project used a staged pressure-test strategy. Each stage attempted to change one major structural ingredient while preserving the previously established distinctions as far as possible.

A simplified trajectory is:

`local/global reconstruction`

`-> Potentiality semantics`

`-> records and directional asymmetry`

`-> stationary constrained quantum relational dynamics`

`-> genuine change of internal clock`

`-> layered O/P/R/V/Xi architecture`

`-> record/Potentiality integration`

`-> typed future-measurement covariance`

`-> external reparameterization`

`-> multi-orbit gauge quotient`

`-> multi-constraint path ordering`

`-> phase-space-dependent structure functions`

`-> spatially indexed local/smeared constraints`

`-> locality-preserving Abelianization pressure`

`-> closed-cycle locality pressure`.

The recurring methodological rules were:

1. distinguish what is locally accessible from what is globally represented or reconstructible;
2. distinguish model-state difference from operational difference;
3. distinguish an invariant or quotient-level statement from a raw representation feature;
4. use negative controls and ablations to test whether a proposed temporal feature is actually necessary;
5. avoid promoting finite-model covariance into general covariance or general relativity;
6. avoid treating noncommutativity, path dependence, records, Potentiality, or relational change as ontological becoming by definition;
7. avoid treating reconstructibility, stationarity, Dirac observables, or Abelianization as eternalism by definition.

The final claim ledger freezes the status vocabulary used here:

- `established_on_declared_family`;
- `bounded_negative_result`;
- `not_established`;
- `underdetermined`;
- `methodological_judgment`.

`not_established != false`.

This vocabulary matters because many of the project's strongest lessons are not universal positive theorems. They are controlled demonstrations that apparently decisive signatures can fail to discriminate ontology on the tested families.

# 3. Evidence trajectory across conceptual axes

## 3.1 Reconstruction, occurrence identity, and accessibility

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 1 began with finite directed relational structures and families of local descriptions. By weakening direction information, coverage, identity labels, adjacency detail, and anonymous neighborhood information, it explicitly separated reconstructible, ambiguous, and lost global structure.

Stage 3 later applied the same distinction to record-bearing trajectories and local observation interfaces.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage1_synthesis.md`;
- `results/stage3_synthesis.md`;
- final ledger A1–A5.

### What survived

On declared finite families, sufficiently rich collections of local relational views can reconstruct a global relational object even when a single view is lossy or ambiguous. Shared global IDs are useful but not always necessary: anonymous relational context can suffice up to isomorphism in the tested search class.

The same project-wide distinction reappears later in quantum models:

`reconstructible != automatically accessible to an internal observer`.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

Several controls showed that reconstruction depends on the information interface and the equivalence relation used to define the target. Missing coverage can create multiple compatible completions or remove an event entirely. Repeated state labels do not determine occurrence identity. A noisy local interface can erase accessible record information without removing the corresponding relation from the global formal model.

Therefore neither successful global reconstruction nor local incompleteness by itself decides whether the world is block-like or becoming-like.

### Strongest licensed claim

`established_on_declared_family`: reconstruction, ambiguity, loss, and accessibility are interface-relative properties on the declared finite models.

### Strongest unlicensed claim

`not_established`: a reconstructible global object is therefore the ontologically fundamental world, or compatible completions are therefore ontically open futures.

## 3.2 Potentiality and the hidden-selected / no-selected distinction

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 2 explicitly separated two model types:

- an epistemic model containing a selected complete history that the local interface does not expose;
- an ontic-extension model containing current Actuality plus admissible future extensions but no selected complete future field.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage2_synthesis.md`;
- final ledger B1–B7.

### What survived

Under matched positive-support conditions, the two model types can expose the same tested current Actuality, immediate alternatives, probabilities, and update trajectory.

Later Stages 8–10 preserved typed modal distinctions and continuation structure inside constrained quantum/measurement constructions while maintaining matched public operational content under declared correspondences.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

The operational comparison is deliberately many-to-one. It erases the difference between hidden selected-history information and absence of such a selector from the model state. Conversely, a zero-support convention can make the public views differ without thereby supplying an empirical fixed-future/open-future discriminator.

Stage 8/9 further showed that retained public P/O/current-R structure does not uniquely determine selected-vs-unselected modal semantics or nontrivial continuation weights in the declared family.

### Strongest licensed claim

`established_on_declared_family`: distinct internal modal semantics can coexist with the same tested public operational content.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: operational equality implies ontological equivalence;
- `not_established`: the physical future is ontically open;
- `not_established`: the physical future is globally fixed/preselected.

This is one of the project's most important recurring results:

`operational equality != ontological equivalence`.

## 3.3 Records, order, and directional asymmetry

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 3 implemented a reversible finite record model with an explicit memory register. The microscopic maps were bijective/self-inverse while record-information diagnostics produced a directional asymmetry under suitable boundary preparation.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage3_synthesis.md`;
- `results/stage6g_synthesis_stage7_gate.md`;
- `results/stage7g_synthesis_stage8_gate.md`;
- final ledger C1–C7.

### What survived

The declared model supports a record-defined orientation even though the microscopic dynamics are reversible. The sign of the orientation transforms correctly under modeled history reversal. The record relation can be transported across declared clock perspectives in later constrained quantum constructions.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

The orientation disappears in controls with no record coupling, symmetric forward/reverse mixtures, maximal independent memory uncertainty, or sufficiently destructive access. The direction is therefore boundary-, ensemble-, and interface-sensitive.

Stage 7 also supplies a no-record constrained quantum countermodel retaining nontrivial perspective and internal-order structure while the declared R layer vanishes.

### Strongest licensed claims

- `established_on_declared_family`: reversible microscopic dynamics can coexist with record-defined directional asymmetry;
- `established_on_declared_family`: mere order is not sufficient for the Stage 3 record arrow;
- `established_on_declared_family`: P plus internal O does not force the declared R layer on the Stage 7 family.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: record direction is the thermodynamic arrow;
- `not_established`: record direction establishes ontic openness;
- `not_established`: record direction establishes phenomenal passage or ontological becoming.

The project therefore retained:

`record-defined direction != ontological future openness`.

## 3.4 Stationary constrained descriptions and relational clock change

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 4 implemented a finite Page–Wootters-style constrained model with a stationary global physical state and exact internal clock-relative dynamics. Stage 5 then changed the physical subsystem used as clock.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage4_synthesis.md`;
- `results/stage5_synthesis.md`;
- final ledger D1–D6.

### What survived

Within the declared ideal finite family:

- the stationary constraint description coexists with nontrivial relational clock change;
- physical reduction is reversible/isometric on the declared physical subspace;
- clock-relative maps satisfy identity, inverse, and composition consistency;
- genuine changes of clock subsystem preserve corresponding tested predictions when states and observables are transformed together.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

Unrestricted kinematic conditioning is lossy. Wrong clock bases can be non-injective even on the physical subspace. Reduced tensor-factor entanglement can change with clock perspective.

Thus neither a raw reduced matrix nor a chosen tensor factorization is a perspective-independent temporal object.

More importantly, the existence of exact reversible relations among perspectives does not make those perspectives ontologically simultaneous, nor does clock change become temporal succession by definition.

### Strongest licensed claim

`established_on_declared_family`: stationary/global constrained descriptions and nontrivial relational change coexist, with reversible composition-consistent clock-perspective maps in the tested finite family.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: physical clock change is ontological temporal succession;
- `not_established`: perspective consistency establishes eternalism;
- `not_established`: perspective consistency establishes a unique relational ontology of time.

`T=(O,P,R,V;Xi)`.

`R=(R_content,R_direction,R_access)`

## 3.6 Typed future-measurement covariance

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 10 closed the previously open future-measurement covariance gate by constructing a fully typed continuation-aware measurement family across genuine clock changes.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage10g_synthesis_stage11_gate.md`;
- final ledger E1–E6.

### What survived

The tested family preserves continuation-specific Born probabilities, weighted predictions, modal updates, and posterior structure across declared clock representations when event/class/outcome correspondence, normalization semantics, and weight alignment are supplied correctly.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

Ablations showed that some numerical data can remain reconstructible after typed semantics are removed. Therefore numerical agreement is weaker than a licensed statement that two charts represent the same operational question.

The Stage 10 result also makes a particularly important blockness guard explicit: covariance of predictions about future alternatives does not establish that those future events are actual.

### Strongest licensed claim

`established_on_declared_family`: fully typed finite future-measurement covariance exists on the declared Stage 10 family.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: future-measurement covariance establishes future actuality;
- `not_established`: measurement covariance proves eternalism;
- `not_established`: measurement covariance refutes ontological becoming;
- `not_established`: typed resource necessity implies metaphysical fundamentality.

`future-measurement covariance != future actuality`.

## 3.7 External reparameterization and multi-orbit gauge quotient

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 11 introduced external reparameterization of a sampled constraint orbit while preserving the internal clock/typed architecture. Stage 12 then introduced multiple physical constraint orbits and a sampled gauge atlas.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage11g_synthesis_stage12_gate.md`;
- `results/stage12g_synthesis_stage13_gate.md`;
- final ledger F1–F7.

### What survived

Stage 11 established finite reparameterization covariance on the declared family. Stage 12 established a finite quotient in which same-orbit representative changes preserve relational/Dirac/typed operational content while distinct physical orbit classes remain distinguishable.

Crucially, nontrivial relational change coexists with gauge-representative redundancy and Dirac-invariant orbit data.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

The reparameterization atlas is not diffeomorphism invariance. The finite gauge atlas is not general covariance or refoliation invariance. A single Hamiltonian-constraint precursor is not the hypersurface-deformation algebra of GR.

The coexistence of Dirac invariance and relational change blocks a simple argument in either direction:

- quotienting does not automatically eliminate change;
- relational change is not therefore ontological becoming;
- Dirac invariance plus relational change is not therefore eternalism.

### Strongest licensed claim

`established_on_declared_family`: gauge-representative redundancy, physical-orbit plurality, Dirac/relational content, and typed descent coexist on the Stage 12 finite family.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: general covariance or diffeomorphism invariance;
- `not_established`: Dirac observables prove eternalism;
- `not_established`: complete relational observables prove becoming.

`Dirac-invariant data + relational change != proof of eternalism`.

## 3.8 Multi-constraint path ordering and basis equivalence

Primary evidence:

## 3.9 Phase-space-dependent structure functions and richer Abelianization

### Phenomenon constructed

Stage 14 made the constraint algebra harder by introducing phase-space-dependent structure-function behavior. It then tested whether the simple Stage-13-style trivialization still worked.

Primary evidence:

- `results/stage14d_basis.md`;
- `results/stage14g_synthesis_stage15_gate.md`;
- final ledger G4–G6.

### What survived

Within the frozen finite/nonzero diagonal no-mixing scalar-rescaling class, the nonzero third-direction coefficient at `X != 0` cannot be removed. This is a genuine restricted obstruction.

However, a richer invertible triangular transformation produces a strongly commuting basis while preserving the sampled quotient, Dirac pair, complete relational values, and inherited public operational content.

### What weakened a naive ontological reading

The scalar obstruction disappears when the admissible basis class is widened from diagonal rescaling to triangular mixing. Therefore the obstacle belongs to a declared transformation class, not automatically to the physical content of the quotient.

### Strongest licensed claims

- `bounded_negative_result`: Stage-13-style diagonal scalar trivialization is excluded in the declared Stage 14 class at the relevant nonzero evaluations;
- `established_on_declared_family`: richer triangular Abelianization exists on the tested carrier while preserving the declared physical/operational content.

### Strongest unlicensed claims

- `not_established`: phase-space-dependent structure functions imply fundamental non-Abelianity;
- `not_established`: scalar trivialization failure is a sufficient signature of becoming.

This stage is central to the later closure logic because it demonstrates the **admissibility problem** directly: whether an obstruction exists can depend on which transformation family is declared admissible.

# 4. What the project established

The final ledger licenses several high-level findings, always at finite/model-bounded scope.

## 4.1 Perspective relations are more stable than raw representations

Across graph views, clock-relative quantum states, gauge representatives, constraint paths, and basis choices, the strongest recurring objects were not raw labels, bare matrices, clock coordinates, constraint presentations, or local state encodings.

The more stable structures were relational:

- reconstruction relations;
- composition-consistent perspective maps;
- quotient membership;
- Dirac/relational observables;
- licensed event/class/outcome correspondences;
- compensated path relations;
- typed operational descent.

This supports a methodological preference for asking what survives a declared equivalence or transport class rather than treating one representation as self-evidently physical.

It does **not** establish a unique relational metaphysics of time.

## 4.2 Global/block-like descriptions and nontrivial relational change coexist

A stationary constrained global quantum description can encode nontrivial clock-relative dynamics. Multi-orbit gauge quotients can retain complete relational change. Several noncommuting constraint presentations have equivalent strongly commuting bases while their quotient/relational content remains intact.

Therefore a simple contrast of the form

`global invariant structure = no change`

versus

`local changing structure = becoming`

is not supported by the tested models.

## 4.3 Operational agreement underdetermines stronger ontology

The strongest example appears already in Stage 2: hidden-selected and no-selected-future semantics can share the tested operational view. Later stages preserve future-measurement probabilities and typed operational descent across clock, reparameterization, gauge, path, and basis changes without deciding whether future events are actual.

This repeatedly supports:

`operational equivalence != ontological equivalence`.

## 4.4 Several candidate temporal roles remain functionally distinct

Order, perspective, records, continuation structure, modal semantics, weights, accessibility, and correspondence/provenance behave differently under the declared ablations. This justifies retaining them as separate analytical roles in the finite models.

It does not establish metaphysical primitiveness or irreducibility.

## 4.5 Basis dependence is scientifically consequential for interpretation

Stages 13–15 show explicitly that presented noncommutativity or an obstruction in one restricted basis class can disappear in a richer equivalent basis while the declared quotient/relational/operational content remains unchanged.

Stage 16 then provides the first significant residual after imposing a closed-cycle locality restriction, but only inside bounded search classes.

The overall lesson is that any ontological inference from noncommutativity or non-Abelianizability requires a prior, independently justified account of which transformations count as physically admissible equivalences.

# 5. What apparently becoming-like signatures failed to establish

The project tested multiple features that can look intuitively like signatures of becoming. None acquired an independently justified sufficient bridge to ontological becoming.

| Candidate signature | Final assessment | Why it is not sufficient on project evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| relational change | `not_established_as_sufficient` | coexists with stationary/global descriptions, Dirac data, and quotient structure |
| future probabilities / Potentiality | `not_established_as_sufficient` | hidden-selected and no-selected semantics can share public outputs; probability covariance does not establish actuality |
| record directionality | `not_established_as_sufficient` | boundary-, ensemble-, and interface-sensitive; no bridge to openness or passage |
| path ordering | `insufficient_on_project_evidence` | compensated path families preserve quotient/relational/typed content |
| raw path-word inequality | `insufficient_on_project_evidence` | raw ordering defects can be compensated without a quotient-level obstruction |
| constraint noncommutativity | `insufficient_on_project_evidence` | equivalent commuting bases exist on Stages 13–15 |
| phase-space-dependent structure functions | `insufficient_on_project_evidence` | Stage 14 still admits richer triangular Abelianization |
| failure of simple scalar trivialization | `insufficient_on_project_evidence` | the obstruction disappears when the basis class is widened |
| failure of declared local Abelianization search | `underdetermined` | Stage 16 search is bounded and the admissibility class is not uniquely privileged |
| closed-cycle topology | `underdetermined` | topology-opening controls discriminate, but no universal topological obstruction theorem exists |
| typed operational descent | `not_established_as_sufficient` | preserving public typed content is not an ontological identity theorem |

The important conclusion is **not** that these structures are unreal, unimportant, or refuted as ingredients of a theory of becoming. Several are mathematically and physically meaningful inside their models.

The narrower conclusion is:

> **t-search did not obtain an independently justified rule under which any of these structures becomes a sufficient criterion for ontological becoming.**

In particular:

`failure to Abelianize != ontological becoming`.

`relational change != ontological becoming by definition`.

`record-defined direction != ontological future openness`.

`Potentiality != phenomenal passage by definition`.

# 6. What block-like reconstructions failed to establish

The same discipline applies symmetrically to blockness and eternalism.

Several structures can appear naturally block-like:

- a complete global relational graph;
- a hidden selected complete history;
- a stationary constrained global quantum state;
- reconstructibility of clock-relative perspectives;
- quotient/Dirac-invariant data;
- complete relational observables;
- future-measurement covariance;
- strongly commuting equivalent constraint bases;
- global Abelianization.

Yet none establishes that all events are equally actual or that ontological becoming is absent.

The project obtained positive finite-family results of the form:

`global/stationary/Dirac/Abelianized structure + nontrivial relational change`.

That combination is compatible with block-like mathematical representation, but it does not identify the representation with an eternalist ontology.

The final blockness audit therefore retains:

- `not_established`: the block universe / eternalism is true;
- `not_established`: reconstructible perspectives imply equal actuality;
- `not_established`: future-measurement covariance implies future actuality;
- `not_established`: Dirac invariance proves eternalism;
- `not_established`: global Abelianization proves block ontology.

Persistent symmetric guards:

`Dirac-invariant data + relational change != proof of eternalism`.

`global Abelianization != proof of block ontology`.

`reconstructible multiple perspectives != proof that all events are equally actual`.

Thus the project does not reach the asymmetric conclusion that becoming failed and blockness therefore won. Both sides remain ontologically underdetermined by the implemented evidence.

# 7. Stage 16 residual issue: closed-cycle locality pressure

Stage 16 is the strongest residual pressure point because it is the first tested carrier on which the declared local search did not recover a strongly commuting local witness even though a global strongly commuting basis remains known.

The result should be stated exactly at its licensed scope.

## 7.1 Positive structure retained

The closed four-cycle carrier validates the declared:

- local/smeared first-class algebra;
- presented-basis compensation family;
- four physical quotient classes;
- strong Dirac pair;
- complete four-clock relational observables;
- typed O/P/R/V/Xi and future-measurement descent;
- destructive/topology controls.

A global strongly commuting basis exists.

## 7.2 Bounded negative result

No local strongly commuting witness was found in the Stage 16D declared local search classes:

1. L0 candidates;
2. 16 explicit one-step L1 elementary cyclic shears;
3. all 69,904 elementary-L1 compositions through depth 4;
4. the frozen 12-parameter translation-covariant affine cyclic L1 ansatz.

The fourth class has an exact algebraic no-solution certificate for invertible strong solutions in that ansatz.

Therefore the final licensed classification remains:

`closed_cycle_local_path_covariant_nonlocal_only_in_declared_search`.

This is a meaningful finite structural result.

It is not a theorem that local Abelianization is impossible.

## 7.3 Topology-opening comparison

The topology controls are suggestive because opening the cycle restores local witnesses in the declared comparison families.

This means that the closed-cycle modification is not observationally inert with respect to the frozen locality search.

However:

`cycle opening changes graph topology != proof that topology is ontic`.

The controls locate a useful structural pressure point; they do not establish the metaphysical meaning of that pressure point.

# 8. Why the residual issue remains underdetermined

The final ledger classifies the cause of the Stage 16 local-witness failure as `underdetermined`.

At least five live explanations remain compatible with the merged evidence.

## 8.1 Genuine structural/cycle obstruction

It is possible that the closed-cycle structure encodes a real obstruction to local strongly commuting reformulation under some physically motivated class of local transformations.

The topology-opening controls make this possibility worth retaining.

But the repository does not contain a theorem establishing such an obstruction on a complete physically justified class.

## 8.2 Search-family incompleteness

The Stage 16D classes are extensive but still declared classes. One can enlarge depth, coefficient families, variable dependence, symmetry assumptions, or transformation architecture.

A larger family might contain a constructive local witness.

The current negative result therefore cannot be silently upgraded to full nonexistence.

## 8.3 Dependence on the locality/equivalence definition

The finite search uses declared L0/L1/Lfinite notions of basis locality. Those definitions are scientifically useful for the implemented pressure test, but the project has not established them as the unique physically privileged equivalence class relevant to spacetime or ontology.

A narrower class can create an obstruction; a broader class can remove it.

Without an independent admissibility principle, the search alone cannot decide which class has ontological force.

## 8.4 Finite-carrier specificity

A four-site graph is not a continuum spacetime. Finite graph locality is not relativistic microcausality, and the finite first-class precursor is not the hypersurface-deformation algebra of GR.

Even a stronger theorem on this carrier would require a separate bridge before it could become a theorem about physical spacetime.

## 8.5 Missing ontology bridge

Suppose a future theorem did establish local non-Abelianizability for a physically privileged class. t-search would still need an additional principle explaining why

`local non-Abelianizability => ontological becoming`.

The current project does not possess such a bridge.

Thus the Stage 16 residual can remain mathematically interesting while still failing to decide the project's central ontology question.

# 9. Methodological boundary reached

The closure decision after Stage 16 is a `methodological_judgment`, not a scientific theorem.

The program reached a point where another bounded search expansion would likely have the form:

`no witness found in declared class X_1`

`-> enlarge to X_2`

`-> no witness found in X_2`

`-> enlarge to X_3`

without a principled reason that one of these classes is the physically decisive class.

This creates a search-family regress.

Three deeper problems remain upstream of simple search enlargement.

## 9.1 Admissibility problem

A claim of physical invariance requires an answer to:

`invariant under which transformations?`

The finite search cannot itself determine the ontologically privileged transformation class.

## 9.2 Finite-model extrapolation problem

The validated models are intentionally finite precursors. Their value is diagnostic separation of algebraic and representational effects. They are not direct implementations of continuum relativistic spacetime or GR.

## 9.3 Operational-to-ontological bridge problem

The project became progressively better at establishing operational covariance, quotient descent, typed correspondence, and basis equivalence.

But improved operational control did not produce a justified rule connecting those structures to equal actuality, future non-actuality, phenomenal passage, or ontological becoming.

Because these three limits remain, merely increasing Stage 16 search depth or basis complexity is judged to have low expected discriminating value for the original question.

The adopted closure judgment is therefore:

> **after Stage 16, the current bounded-search program is more informative when closed by a rigorous evidence audit than when extended indefinitely without a new admissibility principle, full-class theorem, physical embedding, or ontology bridge.**

`project closure decision != physical theorem`.

# 10. Final conclusion

The strongest final conclusion supported by the merged Stage 1–16 record is methodological and discriminative rather than ontological.

Across progressively richer finite models, t-search repeatedly found that features which initially looked capable of separating blockness from becoming often depended on representation choices or survived together with apparently opposite-looking descriptions.

Local information could be incomplete while global structure remained reconstructible. Hidden-selected and no-selected-future models could share tested operational outputs. Reversible dynamics could support record asymmetry. Stationary global constrained states could encode nontrivial relational change. Different internal clocks could be related by reversible composition-consistent maps. Future-measurement probabilities could remain covariant without establishing future actuality. Gauge quotienting and Dirac-invariant data could coexist with complete relational change. Noncommuting constraint presentations could admit commuting equivalent bases. Phase-space-dependent structure functions could obstruct one trivialization class while permitting a richer one. Finite locality on an open chain could still permit local Abelianization.

Stage 16 produced the strongest unresolved residue: a closed four-cycle for which no local strongly commuting witness was found in the declared search families, even though global Abelianization remained available and topology-opening controls recovered local witnesses. This is a genuine bounded locality-pressure result, but its cause and physical significance remain underdetermined.

The resulting evidence pattern does **not** establish that becoming is false. It also does **not** establish that blockness is true. Nor does it establish the reverse pair of claims.

Instead, the project arrives at the following boundary:

> **t-search makes methodological progress by separating representational, operational, gauge, path, basis, and locality effects from stronger ontological inference, but reaches an underdetermination boundary rather than a proof of either blockness or ontological becoming.**

This is not a statement that nothing was learned. The project substantially narrows what can responsibly count as evidence for either ontology.

In particular, the merged record gives strong reasons **not** to infer ontological becoming merely from:

- relational change;
- Potentiality;
- record asymmetry;
- path ordering;
- noncommutativity;
- structure functions;
- scalar-trivialization failure;
- a bounded local Abelianization search failure.

And it gives equally strong reasons **not** to infer blockness merely from:

- a global description;
- stationarity;
- reconstructibility;
- covariance across clocks or gauges;
- Dirac observables;
- complete relational observables;
- strongly commuting equivalent bases;
- global Abelianization.

The final ontological status therefore remains:

- ontological becoming: `not_established`;
- absence/falsity of ontological becoming: `not_established`;
- blockness/eternalism: `not_established`;
- decisive discrimination between the two: `underdetermined`.

The methodological status is clearer:

- the Stage 1–16 evidence audit is complete enough to identify the current inference boundary;
- continued enlargement of the same bounded search is not adopted as the next step;
- the project should remain reopenable only if a qualitatively new discriminator changes the evidential situation.

# 11. Possible reopening directions, without commitment

The closure decision preserves Stage 17 as a historically selected but unexecuted gate. Not pursuing it does not refute it.

A future reopening should require more than greater computational coverage. Qualitatively new discriminators could include:

### 11.1 Independently justified admissibility/equivalence principle

A physical principle that fixes which clock, gauge, basis, or locality-preserving transformations count as admissible redescriptions would directly address the ambiguity that weakened the Stage 14–16 obstruction story.

### 11.2 Full-class theorem or invariant

A theorem applying to a clearly specified complete class would have different evidential status from another finite candidate enumeration or bounded polynomial ansatz.

This could be either an obstruction theorem or a constructive equivalence theorem.

### 11.3 Constructive witness changing the Stage 16 residual

A broader but principled local strongly commuting witness on the closed C4 family would resolve one live branch of the Stage 16 underdetermination.

Conversely, a genuine completeness theorem for a physically justified local class would strengthen the structural residue.

Neither outcome would automatically settle ontology without the next item.

### 11.4 Independently defended ontology bridge

A new argument connecting a validated structural invariant to blockness or becoming, with assumptions defended independently of the toy-model search, would materially change the central question.

For example, a bridge from a physically privileged non-Abelian/local obstruction to ontological becoming would need justification beyond definitional identification.

### 11.5 Continuum or established-physics embedding

Embedding the finite precursors into a framework with established continuum spacetime, relativistic locality, or well-motivated gravitational constraint structure could alter the evidential relevance of the finite results.

A reopening is therefore conditional, not scheduled.

`more depth/degree/candidates alone != reopening condition`.

`Stage 17 not pursued != Stage 17 refuted`.
